01What the module covers

One family, two segments, and a polyprotein that is cut into three by a protease it carries itself.

The module covers Birnaviridae, a family of bisegmented double-stranded RNA viruses of birds, fish, insects and rotifers. Segment A carries the VP2-VP4-VP3 polyprotein and, in some genera, the small nonstructural VP5 in an overlapping reading frame; segment B carries the RNA-dependent RNA polymerase VP1. The two segments are named for their primary protein — VP243 Segment and VP1 Segment — not by letter.

The polyprotein is processed by VP4, a Lon-like serine protease embedded in it, which releases pVP2, itself, and VP3. pVP2 is then matured at its own C-terminus into VP2 plus the structural peptides, of which pep46 is the one this module calls. Two genera, Blosnavirus and Telnavirus, carry an additional X peptide between VP2 and VP4.

That gives nine features: three called directly from the genome (POLY, VP1, VP5) and six mature peptides (pVP2, VP2, pep46, VP4, VP3, X) whose boundaries come from cleavage chemistry rather than from a profile match alone.

The source collections are POLY 403, VP2 398, VP1 379, pVP2 307, VP3 227, VP5 192, VP4 188, pep46 42 and X 2 sequences, after triage of 172 distinct deposited annotation strings.

Two cleavage chemistries do the curation, and they are different from each other. VP4 cuts at A|[AS]; pVP2 maturation cuts at A|[FW]G. Because the two motifs are distinct, a pep46 boundary can never be confused with a VP4 boundary, and each product's termini were checked against the motif its own protease uses.

That test rejected deposits rather than merely sorting them. All 19 IPNV pVP2 deposits ran four residues past the real cut — they end in P because residues 509-512 are SGGP — and were excluded. A 268 aa record spanning IPNV 454-721 sits inside the VP4 length window and is excluded by name, because the window cannot see that it is mis-bounded.

Where a genome's polyprotein was called but a product was not, the product was projected from the junction: 33 members were added to pVP2, VP3 and VP4 this way, each one placed at a junction validated at both ends by the motif above. None was accepted on length alone.

POLY's floor was wrong and the module could not call its own training data. It was set at 400 bits while the weakest genuine member of its own collection scores 316; derive_cutoffs.py measured that and the floor was lowered to 284, which also rescues a fragile 405-bit call in 2556101.4. Eight other proposals from the same tool were declined — notably pVP2 60→255 and VP2 60→244 — because its 0.9 margin protects only training members, and X's proposal rests on n=2.

The length windows come from the literature, not from the collection's own range. Four were corrected during the build: VP4 295→320, pVP2 480→470, VP3 310→325 and VP1 935→1050. 07What it cannot do

Four limits, each measured rather than supposed.

Two mature peptides have a slot the module cannot fill, and neither was forced. In 3081665.5, a complete genome, pVP2 ends at 1664 and VP3 starts at 2397, leaving a 265 aa VP4 slot well inside the 165-320 window — but a scan of polyprotein residues 630-810 finds no A|[AS] site at all, so neither end of the product can be placed. In 663281.3 an A|FG site sits exactly where pep46 should start and yields a 46 aa product, but its downstream cut is A|A rather than the maturation motif, so its C-terminus is unplaced. Projecting either would mean accepting a junction the chemistry does not support.

2920474.4 calls pVP2 and VP2 at the same coordinates. Both come out 201-1385 while the polyprotein runs 36-3419, so both are N-terminally clipped by 55 codons and cannot be separated in that divergent genome. It is one genome and it is recorded rather than patched.

3065069.4 has a 39 nt overlap between VP4 and VP3, which cannot be right — consecutive cleavage products abut. It is a single genome and remains unresolved.

X rests on n=2. One BSNV deposit and one located TV-1 reference. It has no blastp hit to VP2, VP4 or VP3 even at e-value 10, and the BSNV polyprotein tiles without overlap, so the bin is not a fragment of a neighbour — but two sequences is a coverage limit, and X therefore carries no copy_num.

Binarnavirus and letobirnavirus lineages are out of scope. They are not represented in the collections and the module does not claim them.

Use this module on merged whole genomes, where routing is 100% and every core feature calls. The four core features — POLY, pVP2, VP2 and VP1 — carry copy_num and are therefore enforced by the quality model; the five that do not are either genus-restricted or have a measured coverage limit on at least one complete genome.

08What the run found

What the build turned up that was not expected.

A bit-score floor above the feature's own weakest member. POLY could not have called parts of its own training set. Nothing else in the pipeline reports this: the call rate was high, the annotation string was right, and only measuring the self-hit distribution exposed it.

Nineteen deposits wrong by four residues. The IPNV pVP2 records are the right protein from the right virus, off by the length of SGGP. A length window admits them; only the terminal residue test rejects them.

A length ceiling drawn from two genera out of seven. VP4's 295 aa maximum came from Avibirnavirus and Aquabirnavirus; TV-1 at 302 and Largemouth bass birnavirus at 314 sit above it on junctions placed by the measured VP4 chemistry. The same mistake in VP1's ceiling was generating twelve separate findings. Blotched snakehead virus was originally counted here too, at 305 aa; it does not belong — see below.

A quality verdict that depends entirely on which features are declared essential. Applying copy_num to VP4 and pep46 would have turned two complete, correctly-annotated genomes Poor — reporting a gap in the profiles as a defect in the genome.

A VP4 member that was 71 residues of a different protein. qc_cross_feature.py found the projected BSNV VP4 member byte-identical over its first 71 residues to the deposited X peptide. In BSNV the polyprotein is pVP2–X–VP4–VP3, and the projection had been placed on the pVP2|VP4 junction as though X were not there. It sat in a single-sequence cluster and so had its own profile — the only one covering that lineage — so VP4 would have been called on X's start codon in every BSNV-lineage genome. Nothing else saw it: the length audit admitted the member, the profile recalled its own training sequence, and VP4's panel call rate was 99.4%. Trimming the prefix leaves 234 aa, the published BSNV VP4 length, and X's C-terminus reads …LRPQA|A — VP4's own cleavage motif, placing the boundary without reference to either profile. After the rebuild the whole cassette is flush: pVP2 166–1623, X 1624–1836, VP4 1837–2538, VP3 2539–3375.
