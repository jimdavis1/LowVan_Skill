Does the grouped hunnivirus module work?

PICO_LDR_VP4 groups six small genera that share one genome organisation — a leader, the four-capsid-protein layout, a single VPg: Bopivirus, Erbovirus, Mischivirus, Hunnivirus, Tottorivirus and Anativirus. Together they are bovine, equine, bat, duck and rodent viruses that no one would model separately. This is the largest of the four grouped modules and the one where the per-genus picture is most uneven.

Bopivirus 2A is a 212-residue protein; the other five genera carry 12–55 residue StopGo peptides. Split into 2A (StopGo) and 2A_LONG (Bopivirus).

The Erbovirus leader is a 219-residue protease; the others are 51–84 residues. Split into Ldr and Ldr_PRO. Equine rhinitis B virus L has confirmed C-terminal processing activity (Hinton 2002, J Gen Virol 83:3111, PMID 12466488, model-proposed), so Ldr_PRO takes the existing Leader protease (Lpro) vocabulary string rather than a new one.

Two features in one module must never share an annotation string or a gene symbol; the generator refuses either.

112 of 182 routed exemplars get every one of the 11 core features. Features trailing the best-called core protein by 20 points or more: VP1, 3A. Excluded from that count: 2A, 2A_LONG, Ldr, Ldr_PRO — called by an external program and absent from this table, or a protein part of the genus does not encode.

The splits are clean. Bopivirus gets 2A_LONG on 32 of 41 and 2A on 41; Erbovirus gets Ldr_PRO on 7 of 7 and Ldr on zero; no other genus gets either. All four split features are excluded from the complete-genome count, since no genome should carry both halves of a split.

Erbovirus (7 of 7 on every feature) and Bopivirus (32–41 of 41) are covered well. Hunnivirus, the most numerous genus, is called on POLY, VP4, VP2, VP3, 2A, 2C, 3C and 3D but on zero of 76 for VP1, 2B, 3A and the leader. Anativirus misses VP1 and 3A; Tottorivirus misses VP1, 2A, 2B, 3A, VPg and the leader.

07What it cannot do

The gap this page reported has been closed, and what is still missing is refused on evidence.

The projection rebuild this section called for was done on 6 October, and it closed the gap. Hunnivirus annotates every product except VP1 on its two reference genomes and tiles the whole polyprotein, leaving a 234-residue hole between the end of VP3 and the start of 2A. Deriving VP1 from that hole — the references' own coordinates, nothing predicted — produced three training sequences, and three were enough: VP1 went 0/76 to 75/76, 2B 0/76 to 76/76, 3A 0 to 71/76 and the leader 0 to 54/76. Tottorivirus gained 2B and VP1 at 6/6, Mischivirus the leader at 12→21/42. The module gained 309 calls in total.

Every transferred cut had to clear two tests it did not face before. The polyprotein must be at least 80% identical to the reference it is copied from, and the cleavage site must be conserved: P1 is measured across all the references that place a feature, and the feature is projected only if that residue is conserved and every projected cut lands on it. Nineteen feature/genus combinations in this module failed that second test and were left alone rather than filled in — Bopivirus VP4 at 88%, Erbovirus 3C at 86%, Anativirus VP1 at 50% on two references.

The module also annotates a genus it does not declare. Of the 36 Picornaviridae genera no module claims, Boosepivirus routes here on 15 of 15 exemplars and receives 13.9 features per genome — near-complete annotation for a genus with zero mat_peptide records of its own. Malagasivirus and Mosavirus route here too. That is the partitioning rule paying off: these genomes share the organisation, so profiles trained on other genera fit them.

Both genus-conditional splits work and neither feature ever fires on the wrong genus. The projection rebuild of 6 October closed the Hunnivirus gap this page previously reported — VP1 0/76 to 75/76, and 309 calls gained across the module — using only cut coordinates carried from genomes at least 80% identical across a conserved cleavage site. What remains unprojected is refused on evidence: nineteen feature/genus combinations whose P1 residue is not conserved enough across their references to place the cut with confidence.

08What the run found

The run located a fix that exists and was not applied.

The splits were decided before building, by comparing per-genus medians inside each slot, and the coverage run confirms both were right: each form is called on its own genera and never on the others.

What the run also shows is that the grouped modules were built without the projection step that was applied to Avihepatovirus, Hepatovirus, Kobuvirus, Senecavirus and Sapelovirus. Those five had the same problem — polyproteins in abundance, annotated mature peptides scarce — and projection roughly tripled their collections. Hunnivirus here has 122 polyproteins and zero annotated VP1 records, which is the clearest instance of that shape anywhere in the family. Applying it is the obvious next work on this module and is recorded as such rather than presented as done.
