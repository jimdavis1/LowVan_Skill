Does the maize rayado fino virus module actually work?

Marafivirus is a small genus of the Tymoviridae, leafhopper-transmitted and phloem-limited: maize rayado fino virus, oat blue dwarf virus, grapevine asteroid mosaic virus and about twenty more. A ~2,100 aa replicase polyprotein with the coat protein fused at its C-terminus, plus separately expressed CP. 219 unique proteins in all of BV-BRC — this is a thin taxon, and the module is thin by design.

01What the module covers

A separate module from Tymovirus, and the reason is gene layout.

Marafivirus and Tymovirus are both Tymoviridae and would sit together under any phylogenetic rule. A LowVan module is defined by genome organisation instead, and the two differ: marafiviruses carry the coat protein inside ORF1 and lack the tymovirus overlapping/movement protein, which is a 628 aa ORF that overlaps the replicase. Different layout, different module.

02How it was built

252 features, 93.3% binned, and one deliberate refusal to split.

The coat protein is one collection, not two. The source offers 21 kDa capsid protein, minor coat protein, major capsid protein and minor capsid protein, but the p21 major and p31 minor forms are the same reading frame — p31 is the N-terminally extended product — so they are one homology group and clustering can separate them into profiles if it wants to. Splitting on the words minor and major would have made two features of 4 and 5 sequences, neither of which can build a profile at all.

Two of the three features had to be built at -m 2 rather than the default -m 5, because the taxon does not contain enough sequences to reach five members per cluster. That departure is recorded in each feature's BUILD_PARAMS.

RNA-dependent RNA polymerase at 601 aa is an ORF1 domain footprint rather than a separate CDS, and is excluded for the same reason as in Capillovirus: declaring it would create a feature no genome separately encodes.

05Does it call the proteins?

Two of three on nearly every genome. The third is the point of this section.

MP is called on 16.0% of genomes, and that is why it must not be marked essential. copy_num is the field that makes a feature essential, and an essential feature flags every genome that lacks it. A protein called movement protein is exactly the kind whose reputation earns it copy_num on sight — and doing that here would have flagged 84% of the taxon while the module was working correctly. Three modules in this project have scored 0.0% clean that way.

The 16% is not a failure of the profiles. Marafiviruses are phloem-limited and leafhopper-transmitted, and a movement protein is not established for the genus; the eleven records that exist are called, and the feature ships without copy_num and with a comment saying so.

Essentiality was measured, not assumed. POLY and CP earned copy_num at 100.0% and 97.3%; MP did not, at 16.0%.

07What it cannot do

Three limits, all consequences of how little data exists.

The taxon is too thin for the default build. 219 unique proteins, of which 149 are polyprotein. CP and MP were built at -m 2. A module built from eleven sequences will generalise less well than one built from three hundred, and nothing on this page should be read as saying otherwise.

No mature peptides. ORF1 is a polyprotein; its cleavage sites are not documented for this genus in a form that can be located from the sequence, so its products are not claimed.

The evaluation is a sample. 150 of 161 genomes, so almost a census here — but the call and quality rates are still estimates. Routing in §04 is a census.

08What the run found

One feature that would have failed the whole taxon if marked essential.

The MP call rate is the finding. It was invisible before the module ran against real genomes, and it is exactly the measurement that the build order exists to obtain: ship with no copy_num, measure, then mark. Had the module been assembled the other way round — copy_num set from what a replicase, a coat protein and a movement protein ought to be — this module would have reported 16% clean instead of 97.3%.

Nothing outstanding. Two features essential and called on ≥97%, one correctly non-essential, 97.3% of genomes clean.
