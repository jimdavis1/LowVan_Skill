01What the module covers

One circular genome of about 8 kb, read in overlapping frames.

Xipapillomavirus is a bovine epitheliotropic genus — bovine papillomavirus 3, 4 and 6 — causing true papillomas of skin and alimentary tract rather than the fibropapillomas of Deltapapillomavirus. The module is built on genomes from cattle, with deer, goat and reindeer types.

07What it cannot do

The limits, each measured rather than supposed.

E8^E2 is called on a minority of the genus, and that is measured rather than tuned.

Every mature L2 call in this module begins immediately after a furin site in its own L2 precursor, which is the chemical invariant rather than a fixed offset: furin cleaves after R-X-[KR]-R. Across the family 648 of 681 calls on ambiguity-clean genomes cut after a full site (95.2%).

Use this module on complete Xipapillomavirus genomes. It calls 6 core features on essentially every genome of the genus; the spliced features are called when the junction is present and readable, and their rate is stated above rather than tuned.

What the build turned up that was not expected.
