Tilapinevirus

Tilapia lake virus, the only member of the family Amnoonviridae. Ten segments, each carrying one ORF, plus an eleventh protein in a second reading frame of segment 9 that no sequence database holds. The hard problem here was not a sprawling vocabulary but an absent one: 363 of 399 distinct deposits are annotated hypothetical protein.

01What the module covers

One genus, one species, ten segments, eleven proteins.

ICTV ratifies a single genus, Tilapinevirus, and a single species, Tilapinevirus tilapiae. 607 of 619 BV-BRC records are that species; the other 12 are discussed in §07.

Segments 1–3 form a heterotrimeric orthomyxovirus-like polymerase, shown by cryo-EM (PMID 38066000, PDB 8PT2/8PSN). Segment 4 is the nucleoprotein, established biochemically and then structurally bound to RNA. Segments 5–10 have no assigned function and are declared as uncharacterized lineage-specific proteins under the literature's own S5…S10 designations — these are not invented here.

02How it was built

Binning is by homology, because the annotation strings cannot do the job.

363 of 399 distinct deposits say hypothetical protein, and PB1 is the only protein the source names at all. A rules table over the strings separates exactly one protein of eleven. The ten segments are non-homologous to one another, so homology separates them perfectly instead.

The reference set is Til-4-2011, the Bacharach 2016 isolate, whose ten segments are deposited as KU751814…KU751823 in segment order at exactly the published lengths. TiLV-Israel-HK (OQ437054…OQ437063) reproduces the same ten protein lengths independently, so the mapping is measured twice rather than asserted once.

The assignment was validated against the submitters' own labels. 429 of 558 contigs state their segment in the FASTA description; blastn against the reference set agreed with the stated segment in 429 of 429 (100%), and additionally assigned 117 contigs carrying no label at all.

The eleventh protein had to be extracted, because BV-BRC holds none of it: frame 3 of each genome's own segment 9 was translated and the ORF taken at the modal offset of +8 nt from the S9 AUG. 32 extractions, every one exactly 105 aa, 82.9–100% pairwise identity, and all 32 sharing the identical N-terminus MDRKYRFCVSNLDR.

Measured at the annotator's own BLASTn settings, with every module in one database.

Routing was measured with Tilapinevirus, Okavirus and Nimanivirus references in a single BLAST database. A module measured alone has nowhere else to send a genome and absorbs its own misroutes, so the table comes out better than the truth.

The S9 / S9-F3 overlap is the module's design, not a defect: the two ORFs occupy the same stretch of segment 9 in different reading frames. apply_copy_num.py independently recognised this, reporting that every S9-F3 call is out of frame with the S9 ORF containing it and so is a separate gene rather than a cleavage product.

07What it cannot do

The limits, named, with the measurement behind each.

It does not call a protein from a diagnostic amplicon. Of 39 coverage exemplars, 6 called nothing. All six are below the declared coverage_cutoff of 0.65, at 0.23–0.33 coverage of their expected protein — RT-PCR amplicons of 295–420 nt against proteins of 356–419 aa. Restricted to contigs that encode at least 65% of their protein, the call rate is 33 of 33, 100%.

Segment 3 is the diagnostic target for TiLV and dominates its deposits: 151 of 546 in-scope contigs are segment 3, and 71 of its protein deposits are amplicon translations. They were excluded by the length window and the exclusion was then checked structurally — 0 of 71 begin at reference residue 1, and 58 of 71 begin at reference residues 100–124, one primer site, at 98.7% median identity.

Eleven further amnoonvirid species are out of scope. Flavolineata, Piscibus, Lipesoluc and the Catfish, Cod, Flatfish, Loach, Lizard (×2) and Pufferfish amnoonviruses deposit one segment each — PB1 only, and none has a second segment anywhere. Seven fall inside a ±25% window around the TiLV PB1 length and would have trained the PB1 profile on viruses this module does not route, so they are excluded on the species. Measured: 0 of their 12 contigs route here. They cannot support a module of their own, and are recorded as a gap.

No mature peptides are declared. A cleavable signal peptide is predicted for S5 and, with lower confidence, for S6, but neither is experimentally confirmed and no mature N-terminus has been determined for any amnoonvirid.

S9-F3 is not marked essential although it measured 100%. PMID 41513822 names isolates that genuinely lack it — India OM469318 carries a premature stop at codon 50 and India MG873154 has no S9-F3 AUG at all — so marking it essential would flag correctly-sequenced genomes as defective. Those two isolates are not in the segment-9 anchor set and were not tested either way by the extraction; that is stated because it would be easy, and wrong, to present their absence as confirmation.

No splicing, no PA-X, no matrix protein. PMID 41513822 searched for all three and reports none. The probe file tests for them explicitly and ABSENT is the expected verdict — it confirms that no orthomyxovirus splicing rule has leaked into this module.

The literature check is bounded by what was probed. The residual risk is a twelfth protein in an alternative frame of a segment other than 9. PMID 41513822 searched a six-frame translation of the whole genome at ≥9 codons and reported eleven proteins, so that risk is small — but it is not zero.

08What the run found

Defects caught by measurement during the build.

Seven divergent species had leaked into the PB1 collection. A ±25% length window around 519 aa admits the Catfish (606 aa), Cod (591), Lizard 1 (524), Pufferfish (524), Flavolineata (511), Lizard 2 (483) and Lipesoluc (407) PB1 deposits. They were removed on the species, not on length — length cannot tell a divergent orthologue from a long isolate.

A segment floor would have gated real contigs out before routing. The first segments block set each minimum at 60% of the segment's full length, which would have rejected every partial CDS deposit — the majority of this family — before any module saw them, having never been offered to one. That is the Adenoviridae failure the skill records. The floors are deliberately permissive instead, and completeness is judged by which features are called.

The length audit caught its own staleness. Collections were rebuilt after the audit ran, and build_features.py refused to build profiles against a report older than the collections it described.

09What the vocabulary bought

The inverse of the usual result, and worth stating plainly.

Most LowVan modules shrink a sprawling vocabulary. This one expands an empty one. Over the same 21 genomes, the source carries 3 distinct annotation strings and the module emits 11 — a ratio of 0.3×.

That is the point rather than a failure. The source's three strings are hypothetical protein, putative PB1 and PB1; nine of the ten segment products have no name at all in the database. The module replaces one uninformative string with ten named segment products plus an eleventh protein that no database holds, and binds each to a controlled annotation with a citation.
