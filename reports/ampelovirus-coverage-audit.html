02How it was built

Binned from the BV-BRC protein dump, with the accessory names treated as untrustworthy.

The accessories are named by mass, and a mass is not a homology group. Where two masses turned out to be one protein they were merged; where one mass covered two non-homologous groups they were numbered apart.

07What it cannot do

p20A and p20B share a mass and are not the same protein. They are numbered rather than merged, and that is the precedent every later module followed: a mass is a name, not a homology group.

Routing is 89.5%, so roughly one genome in ten of the genus is not reached by the shipped reference set.

The evaluation is a sample. Measured on a random sample of up to 150 genomes at ≥6 kb (seed 29). Annotation costs about 86 seconds per genome, and 150 is ample for the decision it feeds: the test for copy_num is whether a feature is called on at least 85% of routed genomes, estimated to within about ±6 points at that sample size.

The homology rescue has now run on this module. Completed 1 October 2026, closing the item deferred on 30 September. rescue_unassigned.py takes every protein no annotation-string rule matched, blastp’s it against this module’s own collections and adopts each into the feature it matches at ≥80% identity over ≥60% of the query, refusing everything below that rather than sweeping it into a grab-bag. It had never run on this module because it crashed on the trailing tab BV-BRC writes into uniq.seq. 679 of 1185 unassigned sequences were adopted; the training set went 2274 → 2819 sequences (+24%) and the profile set 139 → 147. Its five regressions are one defect on one virus: a profile trained on the 2510-aa ORF1a+ORF1b frameshift fusion of Sugarcane mild mosaic virus matches both halves, so Replicase polyprotein is called twice. POLY was already 27% fusion-length before the rescue (73 of 266); adding 15 more of that virus’s sequences gave the fusions their own cluster and made the contamination visible. Genomes with a duplicate POLY call went 13 → 18 here, against 38 → 22 in Closterovirus. Measured against a control — the shipped module scored on the same 99 genomes with the current JSON — the rescue moved genuinely-clean from 52.5% to 50.5% (3 genome(s) improved, 5 regressed). The earlier comparison against measurements/quality.tsv was discarded: that table predates the 30 September copy_num and vocabulary edits, which on some modules dominate the rescue entirely.

Three features are mass bins holding more than one homology group, and must be split. P7 (five groups: GLRaV-3 47 | GLRaV-1 29 | three GLRaV-13 groups of 3), P21 (GLRaV-3 with PMWaV-6 40 | GLRaV-1 38) and P24 (GLRaV-1 42 | SCMMV 11 | PMWaV 9). No pair of groups shares a blastp HSP at 1e-3. A bin must be one homology group; until these are split each calls two or more different proteins under one key. Deferred alongside the rescue, because splitting forces a build_collections re-run that discards a rescue — the two are one job.

ORF1a and the RdRp were rebuilt from the genome, 2 October 2026. POLY held three different things at once — ORF1a, the ORF1a+ORF1b frameshift fusion, and partials of both — and no threshold separates them: every member has some C-terminal RdRp-like hit and only the long ones have a real one. So ORF1A is extracted by coordinate instead, tblastn locating the reading frame and the walk going to the ATG and to the in-frame stop. On Ampelovirus genome 126071.15 that is ATG 490 → TAG 6448–6450, 1986 aa, matching the tblastn exactly. The frameshift is +1, measured not assumed — frames 1→2, which by the kit's own rule reads as +1, with the subject jumping 6447→6449 so base 6448 is skipped. The partitioning note saying −1 was wrong. ORF1b is not a separate product here, unlike the coronaviruses where it carries NSP12–16: the 524 aa portion matches an RDRP collection member end to end at 99.6%. And there is no ATG for it — the only one nearby is in ORF1a's frame — so the RdRp's N-terminus is made by the frameshift, which by the kit's N-terminus rule makes it a mat_peptide carried by special: transcript_edit with 89 junction-spanning nucleotide references and no PSSM at all.

15 accessory features were added, 2 October 2026. The unassigned pool had never been clustered for this module — UNASSIGNED_TRACKING.tsv recorded it and nothing downstream read the file. Running the kit's own accessory_clusters over it (mmseqs at 25% identity) produced homology groups that a string rule cannot reach: on Velarivirus, 21 sequences all exactly 157 aa with half of them labelled hypothetical protein. Groups that produced no profile are deliberately not declared, and groups measured to be homologous to each other were merged — a first pass ran at a member floor of 5 against the kit's default of 12 and over-split seven features across three modules.

Measured on the same 99 genomes scored after the homology rescue: genuinely clean 50.5% → 50.5%, features called 707 → 991 (0 genome(s) improved, 0 regressed). The new accessories carry no copy_num, so they name proteins without counting against the verdict — which is why the clean rate can hold while the feature count rises sharply. What this does not show is whether the new calls are right. It shows they fire, do not collide and trip no quality check; whether a group is one protein correctly identified is a curation question.

08What the run found

Nothing. This is the only one of the three closterovirid modules that needed no change when it was finally measured, and that is worth recording as plainly as the modules that did.

Eleven of eleven features have profiles, self-recall 97.0–100%, five essential on measurement, and no correction required.
