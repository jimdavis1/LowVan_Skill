Sapovirus

9 of 9 features built · 125 PSSMs · 72% callable

One module for the genus. Two open reading frames, not three: ORF1 carries the six non-structural proteins and VP1 in frame with them, and ORF2 carries VP2. That is why VP1 is declared a mature peptide here and a CDS in Norovirus, and why ORF1 has a sixth cleavage site — NS7/VP1 at E1722/G1723 in Mc10 — that norovirus does not have at all. Best blastp identity between the two genera is RdRp 32.8% and VP1 39.7%, far under the 80% this project requires before anything is shared across an alignment.
