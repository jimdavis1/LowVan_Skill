What was missed, and why

Nine of the ten species are represented by tens of genomes rather than thousands — species E contributes 5 to the entire dump and species J contributes 70 — so their profiles rest on few independent observations and self-recall cannot tell a profile that generalises from one that has memorised its training set. The leftover pass was run on every feature at the same identity floor, with the member floor at 2, so the divergent minority had its own chance to form profiles rather than competing with the species A clusters.

VP8* and VP5* are the only derived features here. They are a coordinate operation on curated VP4, expressed as a motif rather than a column number so they survive a re-clustering, and they are deliberately not projected onto species B through J: species A VP4 is 33% identical to species C VP4 and 19% identical to species B, well below the 80% floor this project requires before a cleavage site may be transferred.
