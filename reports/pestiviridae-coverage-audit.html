Pestiviridae

Does the pestivirus module actually work?

Bovine viral diarrhea, classical swine fever and border disease, plus a growing tail of atypical pestiviruses from rodents, bats, ferrets and pangolins. Thirteen features over one polyprotein — and one of them, NS2, is not modelled from the database at all. It is cut at a cleavage site fixed by protein sequencing, because the database's own NS2 records are too few and too wrong to train on.

01What the module covers

One genus, one polyprotein, cut into twelve products plus the polyprotein itself. ICTV split the old Flaviviridae in 2025; BV-BRC still files every pestivirus under family = Flaviviridae, and the module name follows ICTV.

Npro - C - Erns - E1 - E2 - p7 - NS2 - NS3 - NS4A - NS4B - NS5A - NS5B

Npro and Erns have no counterpart in Hepaciviridae or Pegivirus. Npro is a self-cleaving cysteine autoprotease found nowhere else in the order; Erns is a ribonuclease envelope glycoprotein. That is why Pegivirus, which has neither and no capsid at all, is a separate module rather than a genus in this one.

The dump excludes records under 1,000 nt — 29,157 of 33,605. The median pestivirus record is 248 nt, shorter even than Hepacivirus's 467-nt NS5B fragment, and cannot yield a protein above any runt floor. It changes the denominator of every ‘% binned’ figure here.

NS2 is the feature with no training data, and it calls on 111 genomes. That is 69.8% overall and 86% of the genomes where both its anchors exist — it can only be cut where p7 and NS3 are both called, and p7 is 81.1%.

E2, p7, Npro and NS5A read low for one reason and it is not the profiles. The failures are whole-species and all-or-nothing — every genome of Pestivirus scrofae, ferret, bamboo rat, Norway rat and pangolin pestivirus fails, or none does. Of the taxa missing E2, none has a single E2 training sequence; for p7, none; for NS5A, 19 of 21 have none. BV-BRC annotates the polyprotein for the atypical pestiviruses and does not cut it.

06NS2, cut at a site fixed by protein sequencing

The change of method, and the one number that shows it worked.

BV-BRC holds 69 records under a pestivirus NS2 string. Only 34 are NS2-length, and 24 of those 34 do not end in the right residue. Pestivirus mostly does not cleave NS2-3 — uncleaved NS2-3 is the normal product of non-cytopathic BVDV — and the CSFV reference NC_002657 annotates no NS2 at all.

So NS2 is not modelled. It is cut from p7's C-terminus to the residue before NS3's N-terminal Gly. That Gly is the Gly1590 of the conserved Arg1589-Gly1590 site, established by protein sequencing of two cytopathic BVDV strains (Tautz & Thiel, Arch Virol 2003, PMID 12827468). The motif R|GPAVC is present in 358 of 400 pestivirus polyproteins, and 534 of 556 NS3 records begin GPAVCK.

The same method was tried on Npro and rejected. Cys168-Ser169 is equally well established (Rumenapf 1998 PMID 9499122; Gottipati 2013 PMID 24146623), but derivation gave no coverage gain (120 calls against the PSSM's 122) and the C-terminus collapsed from 96% ending in the conserved Cys168 to 58%. A derived end inherits whatever boundary its anchor places, and only 43 of 100 shared genomes agreed. NS2 works because its anchor is the literature site; Npro's anchor is the capsid profile, which does not place that junction reliably. Npro keeps its PSSMs.

07What it cannot do

Measured limits, none of them fixable by tuning a threshold.

Mature peptides in the atypical pestiviruses. E2, p7 and NS5A go uncalled in 16-21 lineages that have no training sequence for those features. Derivation is the route, and it is available for E2 (anchored on E1 and p7) but not for p7: p7 would need E2 and NS2 as anchors, and NS2 is itself derived — the machinery anchors only on PSSM matches, not on other derived features. That is a real limit of the mechanism, not an oversight.

13 of 172 genomes call nothing at all, and 28.9% are genuinely clean. The panel is species-capped, so it gives every one of 60 species equal weight and deliberately over-weights the divergent tail.
