Five proteins, not seven, and a different pair of them wraps

The bird hepadnaviruses are a separate module from the mammalian ones because they have neither the X gene nor the middle surface form. They are also the control for the circular machinery: in duck hepatitis B virus it is precore and core that cross the origin, not the polymerase and the envelope.

01What the module covers

352 genomes: duck, heron, stork, parrot, Ross's goose, snow goose, sheldgoose and tinamou hepatitis B viruses.

Five proteins where the mammalian viruses have seven. The deposits settle that before any reference is consulted: across 636 unique Avihepadnavirus protein sequences there is exactly one X protein string and five middle S protein strings, against 11,393 and 7,390 in Orthohepadnavirus. All six are mislabelled and none is bound. RefSeq NC_001344.1 annotates neither gene.

This is the point of having both modules in the same build. The mammalian module's joins are P, L and M; this one's are preC and C. The origin is wherever the submitter cut the circle, so the machinery cannot be tuned to one genome's placement of it — and was not.

02How it was built

Identical method to the mammalian module, with a tighter window.

The module declares replicon_geometry: circular. The annotator doubles the contig after routing, searches the doubled copy so that a gene crossing the cut appears whole, and a final pipeline stage folds the coordinates into join locations, drops the duplicate copies and restores the contig before anything scores it.

The segment window is 2980–3120 nt, deliberately narrower than the mammalian 3130–3400, because the genus is: 318 complete genomes run 3006–3048 with a single 3690 outlier, and DHBV is 3027 nt. On a circular module this bound is not only the quality check — it is what the annotator consults before doubling anything, because a partial contig is not a circle. A head-to-tail 2-mer would be ~6,050 nt and is refused.

03The nested pair

Core is precore without its first 43 residues, and no identity measure can separate them.

audit_contamination.py proposes moving 13 core sequences into precore. They were not moved, and the measurement is why: the core collection is 88 sequences at exactly 262 aa and the precore collection 73 at 295–305 aa — both exactly the DHBV reference lengths — and 35 of the 88 core members are an exact C-terminal substring of a precore member. A core sequence scores near-perfectly against the precore canonical because it is the back of it.

Applying the move would have put 13 genuine 262 aa core proteins into a bin whose profile is built at 305 aa, and fasta-cluster-pssm discards a cluster whose consensus is a substring of another's, so the likely outcome is losing them altogether. The same four findings come out of qc_cross_feature.py, and all four are this architecture.

S carries upstream_ext: 0 for the matching reason on the surface ORF: the large form's start lies upstream in the same frame with no stop between, and Met extension would convert every small-surface call into a large one.

04What it cannot do

It is a small module and the leftovers matter. 600 unique sequences across five features, so the profiles rest on 73 to 262 members each. Six of the fifteen profiles come from the leftover pass at a member floor of 2 — five on P and one on the large surface form — which is where the heron, stork, parrot and goose viruses are covered rather than only the duck.

Seven unique sequences carry no annotation string and are left unbound; they are the only row of UNASSIGNED_TRACKING above the member floor.

One contig of 3690 nt is outside the window and will not be doubled, so its precore and core will not be recovered if they cross the origin.

Routing is measured against the family's own references only. Both Hepadnaviridae modules together ship 27 reference contigs and the coverage run was routed against those, which measures recall inside the family and not specificity against the rest of the repository. The acceptance tests were run against the full 1,395-reference repository and both reference genomes routed to the correct module.

All five features are called on every exemplar, all five carry copy_num: 1, and every one recovers 100% of its own in-range collection. The contrast with the mammalian module's precore is the point: there, a third of genomes carry a precore stop variant and the feature is deliberately not essential; here the gene is intact throughout and essentiality is earned.

279 contigs route on 2 reference files, because 70%-identity clustering puts 276 of them in one group and 3 in another.

All 46 genomes score Good, with no flag of any kind — not one genome-level, contig-level or feature-length flag across the panel. The lengths are the reason: the capsid is 263 aa in all 46, preC 296–306, S 166–172, P 784–797 and L 326–376, every one inside its window with room to spare. The mammalian module scores 302 of 589 on the same quality model, and the difference is the deposits rather than the profiles: 46% of those 589 genomes carry at least one ambiguous base and 31% carry more than fifty, against none at all — zero of 46 — here. Hepatitis B is sequenced from patient serum in enormous volume; duck hepatitis B is sequenced from laboratory stocks.

This figure was read from the folded Quality GTOs of the same run, not measured separately. It is reported here only from 25 November 2026: earlier versions of this page carried call rates and no quality figure at all. The call rates were never affected — per-genome feature presence is identical before and after the circular fold, checked on all twelve features of the mammalian module — but the quality figure could only be stated once the fold was known to have run.

06What the vocabulary bought

42 distinct BV-BRC strings collapse to 5 controlled annotations over 947 occurrences, an 8.4-fold reduction. Rarefied over proteins, at the depth both reach (166 proteins) the source carries 28 distinct strings and the module 6 — 4.7-fold. The ratio is smaller than the mammalian module's because the genus is smaller and less re-sequenced, not because the vocabulary works less well.
