Does the rubella module actually work?

Matonaviridae holds one genus, Rubivirus — rubella virus, plus Rustrela and Ruhugu. ICTV split it out of Togaviridae, which makes routing a real question rather than a formality. This is the audit of the module built for it: what it covers, where its thresholds sit, whether genomes reach it, whether it calls the proteins, and what it cannot do. All three species carry the same seven proteins in the same order, so one module is right. The mature peptides tile their precursors exactly, with nothing removed:

                p150 + p90  = p200     C  + E2  + E1  = p110
  rubella       1301 + 815  = 2116     300 + 282 + 481 = 1063
  Rustrela      1082 + 828  = 1910     333 + 324 + 490 = 1147
  Ruhugu        1237 + 811  = 2048     317 + 295 + 486 = 1098

That exact tiling is why the module needs no signal-peptide feature and no SignalP: p200 is cut once, and both structural signal sequences are retained on the upstream product.

The counts are low because the taxon is conserved: 681 genomes collapse to 32 distinct genome types at 95% nucleotide identity. This is the opposite problem to a divergent family — there is little diversity to train on, and little needed.

03Where the cutoffs sit

Seven features, several of them short mature peptides sharing coordinates with their precursor.

E2 runs at 90 bits and C at 100 — low numbers that are safe only because the collections are clean and the length windows tight. The divergent species clear the floors comfortably: Rustrela E1 reaches 513 bits, Ruhugu 596.

The real hazard in a module like this is not a wrong threshold but a wrong extension flag. A mature peptide that keeps upstream_ext: 1 scans back to its precursor's start codon and is emitted on the precursor's coordinates. Every terminus here was assigned from its own biology:

04Does it route?

The question that matters most here, because ICTV split this family out of Togaviridae.

rubella does not route to Togaviridae

The Togaviridae and Matonaviridae modules were deliberately installed in one shared checkout, because that is the only way to test the split. Verified: Barmah Forest virus goes to Togaviridae.7.dna, rubella to Matonaviridae.1.dna.

Four divergent records route and are called but contribute no training data — Ruffle rubivirus A, Eaulepmac virus 1, Rumple rubivirus A and Tetronarce matonavirus, fish and reptile rubi-like genomes. They are not dead ends: on Ruffle the module resolves p90, C, p110, E2 and E1, and its p110 call of 1,055 aa against a source length of 1,054 is a one-residue disagreement, not a failure.

Two cut sites were taken from the literature rather than predicted: the p150/p90 site SRGG↓GTCA (PMID 10799588), observed as ...PLSRGG in 161 sequences and GTCAAT in 87; and the E2 C-terminus, where the PPAY late domain sits at position 278–281 of a 282-aa E2 (PMID 39207105), observed as ...PAYG in 127.

07What it cannot do

One limitation, and it is not fixable from existing data.

Ruhugu's C/E2 junction lands 54 codons early. Rubivirus ruteetense has two genomes in BV-BRC and they are sequence duplicates, so after dedup there is exactly one unique sequence per feature and no profile of its own. Ruhugu is still called, by the rubella profiles, but that junction is wrong. No threshold fixes this; it needs a second Ruhugu genome to exist.

Nine genomes of the 32 near-complete exemplars do not route. The four divergent fish and reptile rubi-like records in §04 are recognised but contribute no training data; if more appear they may eventually justify a module of their own.

Six of the seven annotations were reused from the existing controlled vocabulary — the point of the exercise. Only Protease p150 protein is new. At the end of the rarefaction curves: 13.9 strings per 100 genomes for free text against 3.2 for the module, a 4.3-fold difference. Lower than a diverse family would give, because this taxon is conserved and its submitters were comparatively consistent.

All seven citations in this module are model-proposed and carry the PMID_claude_generated flag; none has been confirmed by a curator. PMID 9557742 (p200 is cut once) and 2214022 (the E2 signal sequence stays on the capsid) carry the most weight.
