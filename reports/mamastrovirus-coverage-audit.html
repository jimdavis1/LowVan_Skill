01What the module covers

One genus, one module, and a genome whose middle protein no profile can reach.

Mamastroviruses infect mammals and are a common cause of gastroenteritis in children. The genome is a single positive-sense RNA segment of 6.0–8.3 kb with three reading frames: ORF1a encodes the non-structural polyprotein nsp1a, ORF2 the capsid precursor VP90, and ORF1b the RNA-dependent RNA polymerase — which is never translated on its own.

5'--[ ORF1a  nsp1a ]--------------------3'
         \___ -1 PRF ___
                        [ ORF1b ]
    [-------- ORF1ab  nsp1ab --------]
                              [ ORF2  capsid VP90 ]
The frameshift was measured, not assumed. tblastn of six deposited nsp1ab proteins against their own genomes returns two HSPs in different frames every time, overlapping by a few residues at the junction, and every transition is −1 (2→1, 1→3, 3→2 — BLAST numbers plus-strand frames 1/2/3, so −1 reads as a wraparound). The slippery heptamer is AAAAAAC.

Four features, and the deposits decided which of them is a CDS.

ORF1b is a mature peptide, and the deposits are what settle it. Of 160 Mamastrovirus records annotated ORF1b or RdRp at 400–600 residues, only 12% begin with Met, and the commonest starts — KKRERGSQGS, GPKKLQRAPE, GSKKLQRAPE — are the lysines of the slippery site. Submitters are depositing the post-shift region, not an independently initiated ORF, so it is declared a product of nsp1ab rather than a CDS.

Length windows were set from the full-length mode of each clean population, never from a median. RNA-dependent RNA polymerase has a family-wide median of 127 residues against a true 515, because the genus is genotyped by sequencing a short polymerase amplicon and depositing it under the full protein's name — 4,332 occurrences, most of them fragments. ORF1B's window deliberately stops at 570: a handful of records annotated RdRp are 1,331–1,417 residues, and those are nsp1ab mislabelled.

A special feature carries no bit_cutoff, coverage_cutoff or kmers, because those describe how a PSSM match is scored and it has no PSSM. 51 of the 54 special features already in the repo omit them; the generator now enforces it rather than leaving it to habit.

The transcript-edit path works, and the proof is positional. Every one of the 17 corrected nsp1ab calls begins at Met — 17 of 17 — which is what a correct reference set looks like. The polymerase, by contrast, begins at Met on only 15% of its calls, and that is also correct: it is a mature peptide whose N-terminus is the frameshift junction, not an initiation codon. Reading that number as a defect would have been the easy mistake.

Self-recall against each feature's own in-range collection: ORF1A 451/451, ORF1B 188/188, ORF2 728/729. Cross-feature contamination: 1,368 sequences against 103 cluster references, nothing above 50 bits.

06Are the calls right?

Two gates caught real defects before installation, and one of them had to be found a new way.

An ORF1A cluster was trimmed off its own start. Cluster 13 mixed two N-terminal conventions — 26 collection sequences begin at an upstream Met (871–882 aa) and 6 at a downstream one (849 aa), the 27-residue difference being MARKGFTSKVDLAVSSGNYNARLQAGN — and the aligner trimmed the long form to match the short, costing the profile 27 columns of signal. This is the defect that shipped in Norovirus at 151 residues.

filter_nterm missed it because its extension test needs an exact 30-mer match and these are different strains. Detecting it by alignment instead found 25 sequences N-terminally truncated by 26–27 residues against a ≥95%-identical longer member; dropping those plus the three short-convention members of the mixed cluster and rebuilding, the gate reports every CDS profile begins at its protein's start.

The reference set got the same treatment. 11 of 243 constructs did not begin at Met, inherited from N-terminally truncated deposits they were anchored on, and the builder now refuses them — the same rule check_met_start.py applies to profiles, applied to a reference.

07What it cannot do

One feature is reference-limited, one evaluation tool is blind to it, and a few short calls slip between two checks.

nsp1ab is limited by reference density, not by biology. The correction gate demands 95% identity; this module ships 232 references where Betacoronavirus ships 3,006. On the one-genome-per-cluster panel that gives 42% corrected and 28% degrading to an Uncorrected nonstructural polyprotein nsP1ab encoding region, which is the documented behaviour between 80% and 95%. Densifying from 182 to 232 references moved it from 35% to 42%, so the lever works and has not been exhausted.

The pool is measured: 327 genomes deposit nsp1a and 145 deposit ORF1b, against the 217 that deposit nsp1ab itself. 48 of the shipped references were built from a genome's own nsp1a and ORF1b rather than its fusion protein, which is what made the increase possible.

evaluate_coverage.py cannot see this feature at all. It says so itself: a feature called by an external program does not appear in that evaluation, and its coverage there is not low, it is absent. Every nsp1ab figure on this page comes from the panel, which runs the transcript-edit pass. Reading the coverage run alone would report a feature that is working as one that is missing.

Four genomes get a second, short nsp1a call of 132–501 residues at a contig edge, against a declared min_len of 694, and still score Good with no flag. qc_duplicate_calls.py reports no exact duplicates and correctly treats ORF1A and ORF1B nesting inside ORF1AB as real architecture, so these fall between the two checks. They are contig-edge fragments of a genome that begins mid-gene, not spurious matches elsewhere, but they should be flagged and are not.

22 clusters holding 37 genomes sit outside the 25-reference budget and do not route. A long tail of singletons is a documented gap rather than a defect, but it is named here rather than left implicit.

08What the run found

Two defects in the kit, both of the same shape as three found earlier.

gen_module_json dropped special entirely. A feature declared special: transcript_edit in the feature spec arrived in the module JSON as an ordinary PSSM feature, so the annotator looked for a profile that by definition cannot exist and the protein was never called at all. This is the same silent-whitelist defect that was dropping PMID and copy_num; internal_stop, translate, non_pssm_partner, begin and end were going the same way and are now carried too.

The converse, too: a special feature was being written with bit_cutoff, coverage_cutoff and kmers. A threshold on a feature with no profile reads as a number someone chose and invites a future curator to tune it.

The kit had no tool for building a transcript-editing reference set, though references/special-features.md describes the construction in detail. build_transcript_edit_refs.py is new, and is self-validating: it scans candidate insertion points and keeps only one whose translation reproduces a protein the genome itself deposits. A genome that cannot reproduce its own protein donates no reference.

09What the vocabulary bought

The largest string collapse this project has measured.

97 distinct annotation strings across 9,066 occurrences collapse onto 4 controlled annotations — a 24.2× reduction, against 17.6× for Norovirus and 12.2× for Sapovirus. The polymerase alone is deposited under 23 different spellings, from RdRp and RDRP to RNA dependant RNA polymerase and pol protein.

Rarefied over proteins rather than genomes: at the depth both curves reach, the source vocabulary holds 33 distinct strings and the module holds 3, an 11× collapse. The source needs 103 proteins to reach 95% of its final vocabulary; the module needs 7, because a controlled vocabulary saturates once every protein has been named while free text keeps climbing.
