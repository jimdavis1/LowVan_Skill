What was missed, and why

TF is now built. It is the one alphavirus product no profile can call: made by a -1 ribosomal frameshift at a conserved UUUUUUA heptamer in 6K, it is not collinear with the genome in any single frame, and tblastn splits a TF query into two HSPs in frames 1 and 3. So it is declared special: transcript_edit with no PSSM and called by get_transcript_edited_features.pl, from 234 curated nucleotide references that each carry one extra templated base; blastn opens a gap in the subject, the gap is filled from the query, and the translation comes out in one frame. Every other base comes from the genome, so each genome gets its own TF. All 234 donor genomes recover their own protein byte-identically, and 236 of 387 genomes get a corrected call. The name of the hook overstates the biology: nothing edits an alphavirus transcript, unlike the Paramyxovirus V and W mRNAs that use the same mechanism, where the polymerase really does insert a non-templated G. Here the ribosome re-reads one templated base, which is informationally the same thing. The source could not be used to check any of this - of the four records BV-BRC annotates transframe fusion protein, one reconstructs exactly, one is a plausible fragment, and two are demonstrably 6K read through into E1 in frame 0. p62/PE2 is also built; E3-E2-6K-E1 was considered and rejected, because signal peptidase cleaves co-translationally and no such completed precursor has been demonstrated.

Three of the thirteen are not binned from the export at all - they are recovered from the genome interval between their flanking calls, because the source holds almost nothing usable. Full-length nsP3, because the profiles could not read past the opal codon in Chikungunya; pE2 (E3+E2), because the export has eight sequences and one of them is full length; and P123 (nsP1-nsP3), because it has two. In each case the interval between two reliably-called flanking features IS the protein. The recovered sequences carry any readthrough stop as X rather than *, because mafft silently rewrites * to a gap and psiblast refuses an MSA containing one - a trap worth knowing about before trying this on another taxon.
