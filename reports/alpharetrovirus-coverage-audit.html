Alpharetrovirus — avian leukosis and sarcoma viruses

avian leukosis and sarcoma viruses

Alpharetrovirus is the avian genus — avian leukosis virus and the sarcoma viruses that carry a transduced oncogene. It is the simplest layout in the family, gag-pol-env with no accessory genes, and the only one of the four retroviral modules whose fourth feature is a captured host kinase rather than a viral regulator.

01What the module calls

Three structural features on every genome, and a fourth that only the sarcoma viruses carry.

src is the fourth gene of non-defective Rous sarcoma virus, 3′ of env and fully collinear — a single tblastn HSP covers residues 1–526 at 93–100% identity. It was never called in the deposited vocabulary; the literature probe turned it up. It is built from 5 unique sequences with a minimum cluster size of 3 rather than the default 5, and the identity floor is untouched.

02Where it does not call, and why

SRC is called on a small minority of exemplars and that is correct biology, not a gap: src is present only in non-defective sarcoma viruses. Sweeping all 294 holdings with nine curated Swiss-Prot v-Src queries finds nine carriers scoring 937–1102 bits and nothing above 69 — a clean break, so the carrier set is bounded. SRC therefore does not take copy_num. Its profile is sound independently of its rate: it recalls 5 of 5 of its own collection, and the real caller annotated it on a held-out carrier at the expected position and length.

04Vocabulary saturation

How fast each vocabulary stops growing as genomes are added.

The deposited vocabulary keeps growing; the module's does not. At the depth both reach, 22.9 distinct source strings correspond to 3.0 controlled annotations, a 7.6-fold collapse. The module reaches 95% of its final vocabulary after 7 proteins against the source's 111.

05Method

Coverage is measured by annotating genomes through the real GTO pipeline — annotate_by_viral_pssm-GTO.pl, then the transcript-edit and splice passes, then viral_genome_quality.pl — not by a reimplementation. Spliced features are included, which the flat feature-table path cannot represent. Exemplars are clustered at 95% nucleotide identity over 90% of length, so one row is one distinct sequence rather than one deposit: this module's 294 genomes reduce to 45 exemplars, and without that step a rate would report how often a sequence was deposited rather than how often the module calls it. Call rates are taken over the 41 of 44 exemplars this module actually claimed; an unrouted genome contributes no features and counting it would understate every rate, and this is the same denominator the evaluation and the copy_num decisions used.
