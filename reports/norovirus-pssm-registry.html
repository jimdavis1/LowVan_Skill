Norovirus

10 of 10 features built · 177 PSSMs · 52% callable

One module for the whole genus. A single positive-sense RNA segment of about 7.5 kb with three open reading frames — a non-structural polyprotein cleaved into six mature peptides, the major capsid VP1, and the minor structural protein VP2 — and no genogroup departs from that layout, so nothing here needed partitioning. The collections are large but the usable fraction is small: the genus is genotyped by sequencing a short amplicon and depositing it under the full protein's name, so 14,837 sequences were refused from VP1 alone, every one of them below the length window.

What was missed, and why

POLY — 25 of 407 calls cover only 17–84% of the polyprotein. These are divergent lineages the profiles match in part, not short genomes: their median contig is 7,371 nt against 7,530 for the rest. All are flagged too-short by min_len, and the mature peptides inside them are still called.

VP2 — Called on 397 of 411 genomes against 404–409 for the rest. The gap is in the profiles, not the declaration.

VF1 — Restricted to genogroup GV by design, not by accident: called on 48 of 49 rodent norovirus genomes and 0 of 362 others. Declared non-essential so the 98% of noroviruses that do not encode it are not flagged for its absence.
