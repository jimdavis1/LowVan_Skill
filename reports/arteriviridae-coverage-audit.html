01What the module covers

One genome, two polyproteins read in three frames, and fourteen mature peptides cut out of them by proteases the virus carries itself.

The module covers Arteriviridae, a family of enveloped positive-sense single-stranded RNA viruses of pigs, horses, mice, rats and rodents. The genome is one molecule of 12–16 kb. Its 5' two thirds is the replicase, read as pp1a and — past a −1 ribosomal frameshift — as pp1ab; the 3' third carries the structural genes, each expressed from its own subgenomic mRNA rather than from a spliced transcript.

That gives 27 features in four kinds. Nine are called directly from the genome: ORF1A and the structural CDSs GP2, E, GP3, GP4, ORF5A, GP5, M and N. Fourteen are mature peptides released from the replicase polyproteins by four virus-encoded protease activities — PCPα and PCPβ in nsp1, PLP2 in nsp2, and the nsp4 chymotrypsin-like main proteinase (3CLSP), and their boundaries come from cleavage chemistry rather than from a profile match alone.

Four are transcript edits, carried as special: transcript_edit rather than as ordinary profiles. ORF1AB and NSP9 come from the −1 frameshift at the ORF1a/1b overlap; ORF1AN and ORF1ATF come from a separate −2 frameshift inside nsp2 (PMID 23043113), which is unusual enough to be worth stating plainly — the same RNA is read in three frames to make three different N-termini.

The source collections are GP5 2,375, GP3 1,981, GP2 1,852, ORF1A 1,805, GP4 1,777, M 1,650, N 1,287, NSP2 964, E 588, NSP3 543 and ORF5A 374 sequences, falling away to NSP6 5, NSP1 6, NSP4 7 and NSP8 9 — 15,898 sequences in all. That shape is the whole problem the build has to solve: the structural genes are deposited thousands of times and most of the replicase peptides a handful of times, because submitters annotate the proteins they assayed.

Where a mature peptide is deposited too rarely to profile a lineage, it is derived on the published cut site and the derivation is recorded in the FASTA header, never inferred from length. 137 PRRSV-1 nsp1α and nsp1β pairs and 10 EAV nsp7α/nsp7β pairs entered the collections this way, each checked residue by residue against the chemistry before it was admitted.

Deposits the source marks , fragment, , N-terminal, , C-terminal or truncated are discarded at intake, and putative deposits go to UNASSIGNED and are adopted only by homology at ≥80% identity over ≥60% of the query — never on the submitter's guess.

Routing was measured against the 25 reference contigs this module ships, of which 25 were drawn on by the exemplar set. Exemplars are the 95% nucleotide-identity cluster representatives of the family's BV-BRC genomes.

All 612 exemplars route and none fail to route. 611 route to this module; the remaining one routes to Simarterivirinae, which is correct — it carries the duplicated minor-glycoprotein cassette and belongs there. It is the same genome the copy_num audit independently reports as routed away, measured from the annotated GTOs rather than from this file.

The module ships 25 reference contigs and the panel draws on every one of them — none could be dropped without leaving part of the family to be routed by a more distant neighbour. The exemplars are the 95% nucleotide-identity cluster representatives of the family's BV-BRC genomes, so the panel is a breadth sample rather than a random one: the rare lineages carry the same weight as PRRSV-2, which supplies 446 of the complete genomes on its own.

The panel is not held out, and the page does not pretend otherwise. All 25 reference contigs are present in it at 100% identity over their full length, because both sets are drawn from the same genome collection by the same clustering. What that costs is measured in the quality section below rather than assumed to be small.

0 of 611 routed exemplars get every one of the 23 core features. Features trailing the best-called core protein by 20 points or more: nsp1. Excluded from that count: NSP9, ORF1AB, ORF1AN, ORF1ATF — called by an external program and absent from this table, or a protein part of the genus does not encode.

The structural set is called on essentially every genome: GP5, M and N on all 612, GP3 608, GP2 606, ORF1A 604, GP4 604, E 604 and ORF5A 603.

The mature peptides of pp1a and pp1ab run with them, which is the point of the module: NSP12 611, NSP3 and NSP4 610, NSP11 609, NSP2 and NSP7A 603, NSP5 600, NSP8 597, NSP1A 593, NSP6 593, NSP1B 591, NSP7B 583. NSP1 is called only 10 times, and that is correct rather than a shortfall — EAV carries a single uncleaved nsp1 (PMID 7769711), so the feature exists in one lineage and the other lineages are right not to have it.

The four transcript edits call lower, and each for its own measured reason: NSP9 518 and ORF1AB 484 come from the −1 frameshift, ORF1AN 429 and ORF1ATF 421 from the −2 shift at nsp2TF (PMID 23043113). The annotator requires a single clean gap run across the whole CDS to place an edit, and a divergent genome carrying any other indel fails that test even when the frameshift itself is intact. This is a limit of the edit placement, not evidence that the products are absent.

Over the whole panel, 569 of 612 genomes score Good and 43 Poor. Across the whole run the quality model raises 21 “feature is too short” and 7 “feature is too long” flags — 28 flags over 13,289 calls.

Because the panel contains the 25 reference genomes, the honest figure is the one with them removed: 587 held-out genomes, 553 Good, 34 Poor — 94.2%, which is higher than the 93.0% over the full panel.

That is not the direction a contaminated panel fails in, and the reason is worth stating. Only 16 of the 25 reference genomes score Good. References are chosen to span the family, so they are disproportionately the divergent lineages — the rodent arteriviruses, the wobbly possum viruses, the simian outliers — and those are exactly the genomes this module annotates least completely. Being in the reference set bought them nothing, because routing to the module was never what they were failing.

07What it cannot do

The limits, each measured rather than supposed.

Zealarterivirinae is a measured gap, and the module says so rather than hiding it. Seven complete genomes — five Wobbly possum disease virus and two WPDV-AU1 — route correctly against three of the twenty-five reference contigs and call the full structural set, but not the 5' half of the replicase: no NSP1 family, NSP6, NSP7A, NSP7B, NSP8, no frameshift products, and neither GP4 nor ORF5A. Part of that is real biology — the 5' replicase region differs in domain content between lineages (PMID 28053107), and ORF5a was characterised in PRRSV, EAV and LDV with no publication placing it here. Five essential features keep copy_num for exactly this reason: these genomes score Poor, which is the truthful statement that the module did not fully annotate them.

The nsp7α/nsp7β junction is unplaceable outside PRRSV and EAV. Twenty-four genomes in other lineages have a bracketed slot, and 19 of 24 end in Glu as the chemistry predicts — but the slots run 104–152 aa (LDV 140, rat 139, bamboo rat 149, Lopma 152), several outside any defensible window, with no paper placing the cut. They were left alone, and NSP7B carries no copy_num as a result.

Three genomes run away and the annotator is right about two of them. 28344.39183 and 28344.39739 have no ORF1a stop codon until nt 11955 and 11666 — a defect in the deposit, not in the call. 11047.866 starts NSP12 about 1,140 codons early and is a genuine miscall in one genome.

Five genomes annotate every protein as hypothetical protein. Seven real ORFs in them cannot be named from the source, and the source-annotation ceiling applies: without literature coordinates they are tabled rather than guessed.

Use this module on complete arterivirus genomes. The sixteen features carrying copy_num are enforced by the quality model; the eleven that do not are either transcript edits, or mature peptides whose boundary is published for some lineages and not others. A Poor score on a complete genome means one thing here: the genome is Zealarterivirinae, and that gap is documented rather than papered over.

08What the run found

What the build turned up that was not expected.

The nsp1α/nsp1β cut site is genotype-specific, and the collections held only one genotype. PCPα cleaves Met180↓Ala181 in PRRSV-2 and His180↓Ser181 in PRRSV-1 (PMID 25557977). Every deposited NSP1A and NSP1B member was PRRSV-2. The profiles therefore called PRRSV-1 nsp1α 72 residues long in 141 genomes — a clean, consistent, entirely wrong boundary that no call-rate statistic could show, because the feature was being called. Reading pp1a position 180|181 across the family gave 125 H|S, 9 R|S, 4 H|A and 1 V|Y; 137 pairs were derived on the documented site, every nsp1α exactly 180 aa and every nsp1β ending in the PCPβ G|G glycine.

Every EAV genome scored Poor for architecture it has correctly. EAV carries a single uncleaved nsp1 (PMID 7769711), which the module already models as its own NSP1 feature and calls 10/10. But NSP1A and NSP1B carried copy_num, so each EAV genome was flagged twice for being right. The third flag was real and had a different cause: EAV's nsp7α was being called at 133 aa against a published 123, eating the first ten residues of nsp7β and leaving a 92 aa slot — and 92 + 10 = 102, the published nsp7β length. The chemistry places the same cut independently: residue 123 is Glu and 124 is Ala in all ten genomes, the nsp4 3CLSP site.

One sequence in 588 put E on the wrong reading frame. Three genomes called E and ORF5A on byte-identical coordinates, and E was the one misplaced — sitting on the ORF5a frame about a kilobase from where ORF2b belongs. All three were the work of a single profile built from a single deposit: fig|28344.22115.peg.9, labelled Envelope protein, 51 aa — which is ORF5a's length, not E's (~70) — and 98% identical over its whole length to ORF5a deposits while matching nothing in E but itself. The bin audit could not see it: its foreign-subset test reports a group only at five members or more, the point where foreign sequences cluster into a profile of their own. But a profile can be built from one sequence, deliberately, so that a lineage-specific protein with a single deposit is not dropped — and a single mislabelled deposit gets the same treatment, with no conservation information to temper it.

Thirty sequences in the unassigned pool were minus-strand ORFs on a positive-strand genome. Three of their four sequence families cleared the five-member floor, so without a strand test each would have demanded a feature of its own. Another 61 were byte-identical to Simarterivirinae ORF5A members — a shared family dump, not a gap in this module.

The audit that checked copy_num had to be audited itself. Its first run reported eighteen non-universal features. Two defects explained almost all of it: it credited another module's calls to this one (2916858.3 routes to Simarterivirinae and looked like an Arteriviridae genome calling 5 of 22), and it ignored the source's own completeness flag (the only evidence against GP2 and GP3 was a genome BV-BRC marks Partial, which clears any nucleotide floor you care to set). Corrected, six features lost copy_num and sixteen kept it.

Rarefaction over proteins: every annotated protein in the 587 genomes both sources annotate (5,187 source product records, 12,771 module calls) shuffled 100 times and drawn one at a time. Both curves start at zero. The source vocabulary reaches 273 distinct strings, the module's 23. At the depth both reach, 5,187 proteins, free text has used 273.0 strings against 23.0 — a 11.9-fold difference. The source reaches 95% of its final size after 4,461 proteins, the module after 102.

The source uses 420 distinct product strings across this family. 346 of them bind, to 24 controlled strings over 25,410 occurrences; 84 bind to nothing and are listed rather than hidden, and ten strings appear on both sides, bound in one genus and unbound in another.

Two collapse ratios appear on these pages and they measure different things. The string-collapse page reports 17.5×, which is all 420 distinct strings against 24 controlled targets. The headline tile here reports 15.1×, which is the 362 string-to-feature pairs against the same 24. The gap between them is the 7 strings that bind to more than one feature.

Those seven are the reason every rule in this module carries a genus column. glycosylated membrane protein is deposited on GP2, GP3, GP4, GP5 and ORF5a; hypothetical protein on four glycoproteins and ORF1a; glycoprotein, structural glycoprotein and glycosylated minor envelope protein each on three. A string that names five different proteins depending on which genus deposited it cannot bin any of them.
