Kolmioviridae -- the divergent delta-like agents

Annotation strings and the PSSMs that call them

The kolmiovirus genera other than hepatitis delta virus -- snake, rodent, bat, bird, fish, newt, toad and termite agents -- make a single delta antigen, not two. The snake kolmiovirus was tested directly, by mutating the ORF2 initiation codon, with no translation from it and no second antigen; no editing has been reported in the rodent, bat, bird or fish agents either. Several of these genomes nonetheless carry an amber codon and a downstream terminator in the arrangement HDV uses, which is why the experiment and not the sequence decides this.

41 genomes across ten ICTV genera, 16 references, 100% covered. The group is sparse and deeply divergent -- the core genera sit at 51-64% identity to one another and 29% or less to the outliers -- so the one feature carries one profile per homology group, and 13 of the 16 are built from a single deposit. Thirteen of these genomes are still filed by BV-BRC under genus Deltavirus; the sequence and the ICTV disagree with that column, and the module follows the sequence.

What was missed, and why

No feature in this module lacks a profile. The limit here is sampling, not curation: ten genera are represented by one or two deposits each, and a profile built from a single sequence can only be as general as that sequence.
