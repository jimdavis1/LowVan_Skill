01What the module covers

One genus, built around parvovirus B19 and its three small accessory proteins.

Erythroparvovirus is the only genus in the module. Six features are declared: NS1, the capsid pair VP1 and VP2, and three small proteins named for their mass — 7.5K, 9.5K and 11K.

Four of the six carry copy_num: NS1, VP1, 7.5K and 9.5K. That two of the three accessory proteins are treated as essential is unusual in this family and is the measurement's doing, not a judgement — both are called in roughly 94% of the panel.

The 11 kDa protein is the one with a described function: a proline-rich protein that binds Grb2. The 7.5 and 9.5 kDa proteins cluster cleanly and are callable, and what they do is not established, so they are named uncharacterized lineage specific rather than given a function they have not been shown to have.

Sixty-one distinct annotation strings collapse onto the six feature names. VP1 draws on eighteen, NS1 on sixteen, VP2 on thirteen. The 7.5 kDa protein comes from exactly one string across 171 deposits — a protein with no function to argue about also has no synonyms to collapse.

Three strings did not collapse, four sequences in all: NS2, initiator protein NS1 (EC 3.1.21.-) and hypothetical protein VP. The first of those is the interesting one: Erythroparvovirus is not described as encoding an NS2, and a single deposit under that name is not enough to declare a feature.

07What it cannot do

Three limits, each measured rather than supposed.

Two essential features are proteins nobody has characterised. A genome missing 7.5K or 9.5K is flagged, and 33 of 300 are. If a clade genuinely lacks one of them, this module will call that clade defective. The 0.85 rule that set copy_num measures how often a feature is called, not whether it ought to be there.

No splice features are declared. B19 transcription is heavily spliced, and the small proteins are made from spliced messages — but each is deposited as a protein often enough to cluster and be called by a profile, so the module calls the product without modelling the junction. A spliced product that was never deposited would be invisible here.

The single NS2 deposit is tracked, not resolved. It sits in UNASSIGNED_TRACKING.tsv with two other strings. That file is a list of unfinished work, not a resolution.

Use this module for erythroparvovirus genomes; routing and calling are both strong. Treat a missing 7.5K or 9.5K flag as a question about the genome's clade rather than as a defect, and check the ambiguous-base flag before blaming the module for anything.

08What the run found

What the build turned up that was not expected.

The capsid labels were wrong on a large scale, and only applying copy_num exposed it. Before the capsid rebuild, 281 of 300 genomes had VP1 and VP2 called at identical coordinates — the two profiles were built from deposits that disagreed about which form they held, so both landed on the same open reading frame.

Rebinning the nested forms by size per species, rather than by the name on the deposit, and rebuilding the profiles took that from 281 of 300 to 1. The deposited name is a claim about which form a submitter thought they had; it is not a measurement.
