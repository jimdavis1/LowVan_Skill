Vesivirus

11 of 11 features built · 32 PSSMs · 69% callable

Feline calicivirus and its relatives. Three ORFs, as Norovirus and unlike Sapovirus or Lagovirus, but with a feature no other calicivirus genus has: ORF2 is itself a precursor, cleaved to release a leader (LC) and the mature capsid. That gives this module eleven features, the most in the family, because ORF2 contributes a precursor plus two products. Pro and Pol are a single product here, as in Sapovirus and unlike Lagovirus.

What was missed, and why

NS1 — The six ORF1 products are FCV-specific. Across 268 genomes carrying the polyprotein, 210 of 212 feline calicivirus genomes have all six and none of the 58 non-FCV genomes has any — canine, mink, SMSV, VESV, sea lion, rabbit, reptile and walrus vesiviruses alike. The product names are FCV nomenclature, so the collections are FCV. All six are declared non-essential for exactly this reason.

VP1 — Called on 77% of routed genomes against 99% for LC, and the split is the same one: LC is deposited independently and covers the genus, while VP1 is derived from an FCV-dominated precursor collection. Also non-essential.

VP1 — Not a prediction. The LC/VP1 site is documented for feline calicivirus, and the motif above was measured over 323 full-length precursors rather than assumed. Two corrections to this project's own notes came out of that measurement: the site was recorded as E157/S158, which contradicted the 14.2 kDa leader in the same line (14.2 kDa is ~124 residues), and P1′ is A in 275 of 323 precursors rather than S in 39. The cut is E124/A125.
