Does the Mastadenovirus, one-fiber genomes module work?

The largest module: 3,821 genomes, about 85% of them human adenoviruses by deposited name, plus every one-fiber mammalian lineage. Core genes are called in 98–99% of near-complete genomes; the routing fix moved 36 divergent genomes back from the multi-fiber module.

01What the module covers

Mastadenoviruses with one fiber gene: human adenovirus species A–E, the great-ape and Old World monkey adenoviruses, and the canine, bovine, porcine, bat, rodent and other mammalian lineages.

median genome length35,253 nt

A species C human adenovirus: E1 at the left end, the conserved core in the middle, E3 and E4 at the right end.

Genomes were assigned to modules from sequence alone: the genus by best hexon match, and one-fiber versus multi-fiber mastadenoviruses by a census of intact fiber genes classed by literature orthology (Podgorski 2023, PMID 36610683). Deposited names were never used to assign a genome.

02How it was built

Membership by homology to curated seed collections; the deposited name is recorded but never decides.

stepeffect
homology assignment12,036 unique proteins assigned (≥80% coverage both ways, best feature ≥1.2× the runner-up)
left out1,284 no hit, 275 ambiguous, 702 low coverage, 9 submitter-marked partial
profiles761 PSSMs over 50 profile features
special featuresE1A_12S, E1A_13S, E4ORF67, K33
mature peptidesIIIA_MAT, TP_MAT, VIII_C, VIII_N, VII_MAT, VI_MAT

Paralogue families were split into orthogroup features by genome position, and each is called by sequence, never by location. Features with no literature function are named “Uncharacterized lineage-specific protein”. Splice junctions come from the literature and were extended to other lineages by aligning donor and acceptor sites; every spliced product was translated and checked in frame.

Self-recall (each feature’s profiles searched against its own collection) is 100% for nearly every feature; the registry page lists every feature.

A genome no rep contig claims is never annotated at all.

Before the fix 3,776 of 3,821 routed here. Rep contigs 12–14, which were the best match for 0, 2 and 0 genomes, were replaced with Lemur mastadenovirus 204, SAdV GZ2-7 and Murine adenovirus 3; a simulation with the annotator’s BLASTn settings moved none of 1,188 correctly routed genomes (smallest margin 837 bits). 3,810 now route here; the 11 that do not are 7 HAdV-F genomes with broken fibers (correctly multi-fiber), one HAdV-F-like genome and 3 divergent genomes the 25-reference budget does not reach.

Essential genes (copy_num) follow the curator’s rules: never on uncharacterized proteins, special features or lineage-specific genes, and excluded wherever a cutoff, copy-number or coverage problem is on record. Essential here: E1B55K, IVA2, K100, K52, PENTON, POL, PROTEASE, PTP, PVIII, TP_MAT, VIII_N.

The subset deliberately over-samples unusual genomes, so its quality rate is a floor for the module, not an estimate of it. Poor genomes are dominated by sequence problems: deposits with no cytosine, N gaps, partial genomes and genes interrupted by stop codons.
