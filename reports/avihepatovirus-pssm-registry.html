Avihepatovirus

Annotation strings and the PSSMs that call them

Duck hepatitis A virus is a single-genus module with one polyprotein and thirteen declared features. Almost every genome in BV-BRC carries only the polyprotein, so the first build had 2 to 5 training sequences per mature peptide. This version projects the cut sites of the few annotated genomes onto the rest, which raises every collection to 18–120 sequences, and it separates 2A2 from 2A3.

What was missed, and why

Thirteen of 196 unique polyproteins are less than 50% identical to every reference polyprotein, so no cut sites were projected onto them. Their mature peptides are still covered only by profiles built from the rest of the genus.

Every mature peptide is a cleavage product of POLY. Its training set comes from the source's own mat_peptide records, plus segments cut from the other polyproteins at coordinates carried across a global alignment. Only cut coordinates already in the source annotation were projected; none were predicted. 2A3 has no collection in the source under its own name: it is 2A2 split at the conserved HLPR motif, where the DHAV-3 reference genome places the boundary.
