Annotation strings and the PSSMs that call them

Matonaviridae is a family of three species and one genome organisation. Every rubivirus carries two ORFs — a nonstructural polyprotein cut once into p150 and p90, and a structural polyprotein cut by host signal peptidase into capsid, E2 and E1 — so the module declares seven features and all seven have profiles. The feature set comes from the literature, not from database annotation: PMID 9557742 establishes the single nonstructural cleavage, and PMID 2214022 shows the E2 signal sequence is retained on the capsid, which is why the three structural products tile their precursor with nothing removed and why no signal-peptide feature is declared.

One module, because the family has exactly one gene layout. What was missed, and why

Two things this module does not do, and one correction. Ruhugu has no profile of its own — its two genomes are sequence duplicates, so one unique sequence per feature, below any clustering floor. It is still called, by the rubella profiles, at 246–2379 bits; but because C and E2 carry cleave termini the annotator may not scan outward from, its C/E2 junction lands 54 codons early. p200, p150, p110 and E1 are called exactly. Thirteen divergent rubi-like records from fish, ray and reptile surveys contribute no training data (one or two genomes each, mostly fragmentary); nine are rejected by routing and four are recognised by the rubivirus profiles with coordinates matching their source annotation. Routing itself has no gap: an earlier draft of this page reported 67 unrouted rubella partials, which was an artefact of measuring with one BLASTn call over 681 subjects against a default -max_target_seqs of 500. Measured against the annotator itself, 668 of 668 rubivirus genomes route at every length band — 217/217 near-complete, 49/49, 19/19 and 383/383 — and the only nine rejections are the excluded taxa, which is the outcome the exclusion decision requires.

The five mature peptides have no collection of their own in the usual sense — BV-BRC annotates them directly, so they were binned from source strings like every other feature rather than derived by coordinate arithmetic. The binning was then proved at the sequence level: in every genome carrying both, the precursor equals the concatenation of its products (114/115 nonstructural, 71/71 structural), and the recovered termini reproduce the published cleavage chemistry independently — p150 ends ...PLSRGG and p90 begins GTCAAT, the SRGG/GTCA site of PMID 10799588.
