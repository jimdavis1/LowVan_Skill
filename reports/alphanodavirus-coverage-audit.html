Does the insect nodavirus module actually work?

Six proteins on two RNA segments, and one of them is a protein that cuts itself in half. The alphanodavirus capsid precursor alpha assembles into provirions and then cleaves autocatalytically, releasing mature beta and the 44-residue gamma peptide that punctures the endosome during entry. This audit asks whether the module reproduces that, and what it cannot do.

01What the module covers

Six proteins across the two segments of the insect nodaviruses.

Flock House, Black beetle, Boolarra, Nodamura, Pariacoto and Newington virus — Alphanodavirus, one of the two genera ICTV ratifies in this family (MSL41, PMID 30431412). RNA1 carries the replicase; a subgenomic RNA3 made from its 3′ end carries two more proteins, B1 in the same frame as the replicase and B2 in the +1 frame. RNA2 carries the capsid precursor and its two cleavage products.

B1 and B2 are translation products, not cleavage products. PMID 15163762 states it plainly — “Protein B1 is in the same reading frame as protein A and thus represents its C-terminal fragment”, “whereas protein B2 is in the +1 reading frame relative to protein A” — so both are declared as CDS rather than mature peptides, and both initiate at their own AUG on subgenomic RNA3.

02How it was built

51 training sequences in six collections, three of them extracted from genomes rather than from deposits.

The genus has 31 genomes in BV-BRC and 54 protein records between them, so the collections are small by nature. Three features could not be built from deposits at all and were extracted from the genomes, each under a structural gate that the deposits alone could never supply.

The cleavage site was measured, not read off a residue number. The literature gives FHV's site as Asn363‖Ala364 (PMID 1404613; N363D/T/A abolish both cleavage and infectivity, and all nine revertants restored Asn). But alpha runs 395–407 aa across the genus, so quoting 363 would misplace the cut in every other species. Each precursor was instead scanned for Asn followed by Ala or Ser with 38–52 residues remaining. That finds exactly one site in every species and two in none:

The scan reproduces FHV's published Asn363‖Ala364 without being given it, and recovers Pariacoto as the one documented exception — Asn‖Ser, gamma 40 aa (PMID 10799587: “the amino acid sequence at the cleavage site, which is Asn/Ala in all other alphanodaviruses, is Asn/Ser in PaV”).

03Where the cutoffs sit

Left at the generated floors, deliberately, and that is a limitation rather than an oversight.

The project rule is to raise each cutoff to half the lowest score among a feature's genuine panel calls, capped at 500 — and to leave alone any feature with fewer than five panel calls, because one low score is not a distribution. Only four complete held-out genomes exist for this genus, so every feature here falls under that floor and keeps its generated cutoff.

Routing was measured at the annotator's own BLASTn settings (-evalue 0.5 -reward 2 -penalty -3 -word_size 11 -soft_masking false, floor 150 bits), not with check_rep_contigs.py, which calls bare blastn — that is megablast, and it finds almost nothing below about 85% identity.

The module-only routing repository was justified by measurement, not assumed. Every in-scope nodavirus genome was BLASTed against all 1,466 reference files in the runtime. The best hit to any non-nodavirus module is 45 bits (Kobuvirus), against a routing floor of 150. There are no neighbours to misroute to.

Across all 26 genomes that carry any call, the six features land at 54–62% — but that denominator is dominated by single-segment deposits, and a genome holding only RNA2 cannot call a protein encoded on RNA1. Measured on the four genomes that carry both complete segments, the replicase and the whole capsid cassette are called every time.

The single genome missing B1 and B2 is Alphanodavirus HB-2007/CHN (756850.3), and it is complete. That is why neither feature is marked essential: one complete genome failing to call a feature is enough to withhold copy_num, because the flag would otherwise report a profile gap as a genome defect.

06Are the calls right?

Coordinates against GenBank, and the test that matters most for a self-cleaving capsid.

Flock House virus RNA1 (NC_004146) and RNA2 (NC_004144) are called at exactly the coordinates GenBank annotates:

  RNA1   A      40-3036     998 aa     GenBank  40..3036
         B1   2728-3036     102 aa     GenBank  2728..3036
         B2   2738-3058     106 aa     GenBank  2738..3058

  RNA2   alpha  23-1246     407 aa
         beta   23-1111     363 aa     literature 363 (PMID 9765417)
         gamma 1112-1246      44 aa    literature  44

The tiling test, over every panel genome. A precursor and its products must add up: beta must start where alpha starts, gamma must end where alpha ends, and gamma must begin one base after beta ends. Any drift at a cleaved terminus shows up here and nowhere else.

  beta starts at alpha's start      4 / 4
  gamma ends at alpha's stop        4 / 4
  gamma starts 1 nt after beta      4 / 4   (no gap, no overlap)
No creep at either cleaved terminus. upstream_ext is 1 on beta (it begins at alpha's own start codon) and 0 on gamma; downstream_ext is 0 on beta (a protease cut has no codon to find) and 1 on gamma (it runs to alpha's real stop). Getting either backwards is what produces the drift this test looks for.

One defect was found this way and fixed. B1 lies entirely inside the protein A reading frame, so there is no stop codon upstream for the annotator's scan_to_met_start to halt on. With upstream_ext: 1 the scan walked left through protein A to an arbitrary Met and called B1 at 2620..3036 = 138 aa, 36 residues too long. Setting it to 0 — the profile's own match extent decides the start — produced the 102 aa call above.

07What it cannot do

Three limits, and none of them is a threshold problem.

1. B1 is callable only where its start codon can be placed. The collection holds four members from three species. B1's start is published for FHV and Black beetle virus (GenBank) and for Nodamura (protein A residue 908, PMID 15163762), and for nobody else; 8 of 13 candidate genomes were rejected because the position aligned to the reference's first residue is not a Met. An earlier build accepted the nearest Met within 40 codons instead and produced members of 102, 112, 131 and 138 aa from one gene. A thin collection of correct starts is worth more than a full one of invented ones, and B1 is not marked essential.

2. The Pariacoto minor capsid protein is not modelled, and cannot be. It is a real virion protein, translated from the second AUG of the capsid ORF and 24 residues shorter than alpha (PMID 13679619). Being alpha minus an N-terminal run, it is 100% identical to alpha over its entire length — no profile can separate the two. This is the nested alternative-start trap the LowVan skill documents for parvovirus VP1/VP2, and the only honest treatment is to declare it absent.

3. The Black beetle virus RNA2 second ORF is not modelled. PMID 6548308 predicted “a second open reading frame for a putative protein 72 amino acid residues long” at base 1110 in 1984. No product has been reported in the four decades since and one deposit exists family-wide. It is excluded by name, not by a length window, so the decision is visible.

And the panel is small. Four complete held-out genomes is enough to check correctness and too few to set cutoffs or to resolve B1's 75% call rate. Only 399 of 1,965 isolate names in this family have both segments deposited, and this genus has 31 genomes in total; that is a property of the deposits, not something the module can fix.

Every nesting the duplicate-call check reports is declared genome architecture: B1 and B2 inside protein A, beta and gamma inside alpha. There are no exact duplicates and no undeclared nesting in any module of this family.

A panel genome here had to clear three conditions: it carries both segments, the two contigs are different segments, and it is not a reference. The second is not redundant — merge_segments.py merges any two records sharing a genome name, and in sibling modules it produced “genomes” made of two copies of the same segment. Each contig is therefore assigned by homology to the module's own collections rather than by length or by the FASTA description.

The source vocabulary for these six proteins includes protein A, put. protein A (m.w. 112 kD), RNA dependent RNA polymerase protein A and putative polymerase for the replicase alone, and coat protein precursor, capsid protein precursor alpha, capsid precursor protein alpha, protein alpha and a bare hypothetical protein for the capsid precursor.

Three of the six strings are new to the LowVan vocabulary — Non-structural protein B1, Mature capsid protein beta and Capsid peptide gamma. The replicase reuses RNA-dependent RNA polymerase, the precursor reuses Capsid protein precursor, and B2 reuses RNA silencing suppressor protein — a claim this genus has earned, since alphanodavirus B2 is the founding animal-virus RNAi suppressor (PMID 12016316).
