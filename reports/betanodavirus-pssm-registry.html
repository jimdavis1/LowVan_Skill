Betanodavirus — the fish nervous necrosis viruses

Annotation strings and the PSSMs that call them

The fish nervous necrosis viruses. Four proteins on two segments, and the headline fact is a negative one: unlike the insect nodaviruses, the betanodavirus capsid is not cleaved. Its RNA2 ORF “carries the catalytic D-75 residue but lacks the site for autocatalytic proteolysis” (PMID 9672600), so no beta and no gamma are declared here.

What was missed, and why

Beta and gamma are probed for and come back ABSENT, which is the required result rather than a shortfall: it proves the alphanodavirus cleavage rule did not leak across into a genus that does not use it. protein C and protein D are single deposits using positional letters that name no homology group, and are left to the homology rescue.
