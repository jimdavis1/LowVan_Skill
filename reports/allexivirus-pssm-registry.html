TGB3 has one profile built from three sequences.

All three are isolates of a single virus, Allexivirus sigmamedicagonis, and that one profile calls TGB3 on 101 of 103 genomes. The gene is real — allexiviruses initiate it at a CUG (Lezzhov 2015, PMID 26296665) — but the evidence base behind the profile is the thinnest in either module, and its 5′ coordinate is approximate because the CUG cannot be located mechanically.

Two further Allexivirus TGB3 proteins exist in the source data, from Blackberry calico virus (89 aa) and Garlic virus C AS16 (94 aa). Neither clustered, so neither contributes. Recovering them is the cheapest available improvement to this module.

Why these thresholds

upstream_ext is set per feature from measurement, not policy. Each firing of scan_to_met_start was recorded as finding a Met or running to the previous in-frame stop: ALLEXI_40K 19:4, CP 12:2 and TGB2/NABP never firing keep the scan enabled; REP 1:4, TGB1 0:18 and TGB3 2:190 have it disabled.

TGB3's length gate is deliberately loose at 40–130 against a training distribution that is a single value of 98. Because upstream_ext: 0 anchors the call at the homology boundary rather than the true CUG, a gate tightened to the training data would flag the coordinate rather than the genome.

Composition

Every profile is Allexivirus-only except TGB1.9, which is 20 of 21 (95%). Both the parent and this module retain these sequences: a split does not delete profiles from the parent, so Alphaflexiviridae still carries all 1,321 in its own profiles.

NABP is worth noting for the opposite reason to the parent module: here its 338 sequences are all Allexivirus and it is called on 65% of genomes, whereas in Alphaflexiviridae the same 338 sequences dominate a feature called on 3% of genomes.

Profile identifiers

Allexivirus.<FEATURE>.<n>, integers throughout. These profiles kept the numbers they were renumbered to in the parent before the split, so TGB3.25 here is the profile that was TGB3.lo7 and then TGB3.25 in Alphaflexiviridae. The ids are written into every annotated genome's family_assignments, which is why the loN prefix was removed.
