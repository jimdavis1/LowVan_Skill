Pegivirus

A controlled vocabulary stops growing. Free text does not.

A Pegivirus genome encodes the same 9 modelled proteins as any other, so a curated vocabulary needs exactly 9 names no matter how many genomes you read. Free-text product strings have no such ceiling: each new submitter spells the same proteins a new way. Sampling 116 annotated genomes 100 times over shows the two curves doing exactly that — the module is saturated after 2 genomes, while the source vocabulary is still climbing at 116.

9strings, controlled vocabulary
48strings, BV-BRC free text
5.3×collapse per 100 genomes
103genomes before free text saturates

The ratio is the number worth quoting: at the end of each curve, free text costs 41.4 strings per 100 genomes and the controlled vocabulary 7.8 — a 5.3-fold collapse. Reaching 95% of its final size takes the source vocabulary 103 genomes (89% of the set); the module gets there on genome 2.
