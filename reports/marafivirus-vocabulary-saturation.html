Marafivirus

A controlled vocabulary stops growing. Free text does not.

A Marafivirus genome encodes the same 3 modelled proteins as any other, so a curated vocabulary needs exactly 3 names no matter how many genomes you read. Free-text product strings have no such ceiling: each new submitter spells the same proteins a new way. Sampling 144 annotated genomes 100 times over shows the two curves doing exactly that — the module is saturated after 10 genomes, while the source vocabulary is still climbing at 144.

3strings, controlled vocabulary
20strings, BV-BRC free text

The ratio is the number worth quoting: at the end of each curve, free text costs 13.9 strings per 100 genomes and the controlled vocabulary 2.1 — a 6.7-fold collapse. Reaching 95% of its final size takes the source vocabulary 115 genomes (80% of the set); the module gets there on genome 10.
