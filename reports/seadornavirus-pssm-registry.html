What was missed, and why

Every feature earned copy_num: eleven are called in 100% of the held-out panel and VP12 in 97.5%. That is the easiest result in the family and it should be read against the module's size rather than as a claim of robustness — 696 sequences across four species, with Banna virus supplying half of them. The profiles for Mangshi virus, which contributes 36 records to the entire dump, rest on very few independent observations, and self-recall cannot distinguish a profile that generalises from one that has memorised its training set.
