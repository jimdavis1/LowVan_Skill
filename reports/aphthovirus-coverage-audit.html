Does the foot-and-mouth disease virus module work?

Aphthovirus is the genus of foot-and-mouth disease virus, the most economically consequential livestock pathogen there is: an outbreak closes borders, and the 2001 British epidemic cost some 6 million animals. The genus also holds the equine and bovine rhinitis viruses. This audit covers the module built for it — what it covers, how it was built, where the cutoffs sit, whether genomes reach it, whether it calls the proteins, and the one place where it systematically did not until it was fixed.

01What the module covers

One genus, four species, and a genome layout that is identical everywhere except at 3B.

The aphthovirus polyprotein is a leader protease, VP4, VP2, VP3, VP1, a 2A of 18 residues that separates the capsid precursor by ribosome skipping rather than proteolysis, then 2B, 2C, 3A, VPg, 3C and 3D. The leader is a genuine papain-like protease of about 201 residues, which is why this genus is a separate module from Cardiovirus, whose leader slot holds a 71-residue zinc-finger protein instead.

13,159 unique sequences from 4,296 genomes makes this the best-sampled genus in the family, and every feature built at -m 5 -mi 0.8 without a departure. Each measured median matched the published FMDV size: leader 201, VP4 85, VP2 218, VP3 220, VP1 211, 2A 18, 2C 318, 3A 153, 3C 213, 3D 470. Nothing in the binning rules knows about length, so that agreement is an independent check that the triage was right.

VPg was set to the FMDV tandem deliberately. FMDV carries three tandem copies of 3B, and 410 BV-BRC records annotate all three as a single 3B1/3B2/3B3 mat_peptide of 71 residues (23 + 24 + 24). Three profiles built from mutually paralogous 23-residue peptides would never separate, so the module models the tandem as one feature. That decision is correct for FMDV and is the direct cause of the gap in section 07.

07What it cannot do

One genome, and the limits of a tandem.

Coypu aphthovirus has no VPg call. It is a single genome and BV-BRC holds no VPg record for it, so there is nothing to build a profile from. That is the entire residual gap in this feature: 422 of 431, with the nine misses being eight FMDV genomes and this one.

The module models the FMDV tandem as a single feature, and that remains a modelling choice rather than a biological claim. FMDV carries three copies of 3B and 410 BV-BRC records annotate all three as one 71-residue mat_peptide. Three profiles built from mutually paralogous 23-residue peptides would never separate, so VPG models the tandem. The three copies are individually real and individually functional — all three are uridylylated by 3Dpol — and the module does not report them separately.

What the module cannot do is tell you which copy it found. On a genome where only one copy is present it calls that copy; on FMDV it calls the tandem. Both are reported with the same annotation string. A user wanting 3B1, 3B2 and 3B3 as separate features would need them derived positionally from the tandem call, which has not been done.

Twelve features are right across the whole genus. The thirteenth is right for FMDV and absent for the other three species, costing 49 of 431 exemplars, and it is a partitioning decision rather than a tuning problem. The fix is one additional feature for the single-copy VPg, and it needs a cross-feature measurement first because the FMDV tandem contains the single-copy form.

08What the run found

The run found a partitioning error, and fixing it took three attempts.

The error. VPg's collection was 443 FMDV tandem sequences of 71 residues against 8 of anything else, and the window that follows from it was 57–85. Counting every VPg record by species settles what that cost: A. vesiculae contributes 2,209 records of 71 residues and no single copies; A. reedi, A. bogeli and A. burrowsi contribute 92 records of 23–25 residues and no tandems. The two populations do not overlap. A 24-residue VPg could not clear a 57-residue floor at any bit cutoff, so the module did not fail to score those proteins — it declined to consider them.

First attempt, wrong design. The obvious fix is a second feature for the single-copy form. It is wrong: of 50 unique single-copy sequences, 38 are FMDV — submitters annotating 3B1/3B2/3B3 separately — and every aphthovirus VPg copy begins GPY, so a 24-mer profile necessarily matches the tandem's first 24 residues. Two features would have competed at one locus, which is the truncated-profile case the curation checklist forbids.

What worked. One feature covering both forms. The 50 single-copy sequences were merged in (451 → 501) and the window widened to 19–85; the clustering separated the two forms by itself, into two profiles of 71 residues and two of 23–25. The cutoff was then measured rather than guessed: tandem profiles score 135–147 on FMDV and 30–36 on the rhinitis viruses, single-copy profiles 51–56 on the rhinitis viruses and 21–26 on FMDV. Noise ceiling 36, weakest true signal 49 → bit_cutoff 42. The previous cutoff of 31 was itself wrong: it would let a 71-residue tandem profile fire on a 24-residue protein.

And one species needed a third step. A. burrowsi stayed at 0 of 5 after all that. Its two VPg sequences were already in the collection but start SAYDP where every other aphthovirus VPg starts GPY, and two divergent sequences reach no cluster in the main pass. They came in through the leftover pass at its member floor of two, with the length floor set relative to the feature median so that 24-mers in a 71-mer collection are not excluded. One more profile, scoring 49 on burrowsi and 19 on FMDV.

Result: A. reedi 20/32 → 32/32, A. bogeli 2/23 → 23/23, A. burrowsi 0/5 → 5/5, FMDV unchanged at 360/368, and no genome anywhere with a duplicate VPg call — the specific risk the measured cutoff was guarding against.

Both curves start at zero. The source vocabulary reaches 101 distinct strings, the module's 13. At the depth both reach, 4,801 proteins, free text has used 101.0 strings against 13.0 — a 7.8-fold difference. The source reaches 95% of its final size after 4,045 proteins, the module after 40.

Aphthovirus is the most heavily submitted genus here, and its free text is correspondingly varied: 53 source strings bind to no feature at all and the bound ones collapse at 10.9×, the highest ratio of the ten picornavirus modules. Drawn one protein at a time, the module's vocabulary is fixed within the first few dozen proteins while the source vocabulary is still climbing at the end of the set.
