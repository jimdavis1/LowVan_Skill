What the annotation cleanup actually collapsed

115 distinct BV-BRC product strings collapse to 11 controlled annotations across 123,591 protein features. Eleven, not the module’s full fourteen, and the gap is the point: pE2, P123 and TF absorb no source strings at all, because the export holds 8 sequences for one, 2 for the other, and for TF 4 records of which 2 are mis-annotated 6K–E1 fusions. The first two are recovered from the genome interval between their flanking calls and TF is reconstructed from its frameshift, so all three add vocabulary without removing any. Measured a second way, on 387 near-complete genomes with the vocabulary accumulated in random order, the source is still climbing at 80 strings while the module has saturated.
