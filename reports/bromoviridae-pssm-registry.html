What was missed, and why

Routing across the family is 74.2% of complete genomes, re-measured on 30 September 2026 because neither figure reported before could be substantiated: 94.8% came from merging records by genome name, which collapses isolate collisions as well as segments, and 69.5% had no computation behind it. A three-way split would reach about 90% (Cucumovirus 95.5%, Ilarvirus 74.1%, Alfamovirus-group 100%) and has not been acted on.
