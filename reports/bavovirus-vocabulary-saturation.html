Bavovirus Vocabulary Saturation

Every other module in this series answers the vocabulary question with a rarefaction curve. Bavovirus is too small for one, and that is the finding on this page rather than a reason to omit it.

No curve was drawn. only 26 protein(s) are annotated by both the source and the module, below the floor of 40 needed for a rarefaction curve to mean anything

A rarefaction asks how fast distinct annotation strings accumulate as proteins are drawn. With a pool this small the curve would be a handful of points and its plateau an artefact of running out of proteins, not evidence that a vocabulary had saturated.
