Why the split was forced

One feature needed upstream_ext: 1 in one genus and 0 in the other.

Not a tidiness exercise. A module carries one setting per feature, so this could not be expressed without separating the genera.

upstream_ext permits the annotator to scan upstream for a Met when a PSSM match does not begin with one. To decide it per feature rather than by policy, scan_to_met_start was instrumented across all 273 annotated genomes and every firing recorded as either finding a Met or running to the previous in-frame stop.

TGB3 scores 38 found against 10 missed here. The same feature in Allexivirus scores 2 against 190, because allexivirus TGB3 initiates at a CUG rather than an AUG — there is no Met to find, so the scan runs to the previous stop and emits a CDS roughly 35 residues too long, overlapping TGB2. Setting the field either way was wrong for one genus.

Two results here would have been missed by setting this from policy rather than measurement: TGB1 and REP fail in both genera and are now 0 in both modules. A blanket "leave it on" would have kept 24 misfires; a blanket "turn it off" would have cost 40 correct initiators on TGB3 alone.

NABP is rare but correct, and the profiles are partitioned by genus. It is called on 4 of the 123 genomes in the post-split run, and every call is a Mandarivirus — Indian citrus ringspot, Citrus yellow vein clearing, Citrus yellow mottle and White kwao krua mandarivirus 1, at 220–223 aa. Each was called by a Mandarivirus-trained profile: NABP.5 is 38 Citrus yellow vein clearing sequences at 222 aa, NABP.14_1 is 9 Indian citrus ringspot and relatives at 178 aa. The Allexivirus-trained profiles are a separate 127-aa class and fire on nothing, because garlic and shallot genomes route to the Allexivirus module now. So the two genera do not mix within a profile and the retained Allexivirus training data cannot distort a call — it is dormant weight, not contamination. An earlier version of this page called NABP a loose end and suggested rebuilding it from Potexvirus sequences alone. That was wrong twice over: there is no Potexvirus NABP in the collection at all, and the Mandarivirus profiles that do exist are already doing the right thing. Curator's decision, 22 September 2026: the calls are correct, so the feature is kept as it is.

Runaway upstream Met scanning

A property of the annotator, retained deliberately by the curator. Recorded because it shapes the numbers above, not as a proposal to change it.

scan_to_met_start is documented as searching left "until it finds the first" Met. Its loop advances the candidate start one codon at a time and exits on either a Met or an in-frame stop, but the stop branch exits without restoring the original coordinate — so when there is no upstream Met it returns the codon after the previous stop and the CDS runs to the edge of the upstream ORF.

The measurement above is the reason this is now a minor issue here rather than a major one: with upstream_ext set per feature from evidence, the scan fires where it succeeds and is disabled where it does not. Residual non-Met starts in this module total 33 of 848 calls (3.9%), concentrated in TGB1 (16) and REP (7), both of which are already 0 and are therefore anchored at the homology boundary rather than over-extended.

Provenance

259 profiles against 259 alignments, verified by direct comparison, no feature mismatched. Leftover profiles carry plain integers; the loN prefix that leaked a build-pipeline detail into family_assignments has been removed across the repository and the kit.

The module declares six features. ALLEXI_40K was removed when Allexivirus left, because it is 100% Allexivirus and declaring a protein the module's taxa do not encode is worse than omitting it; its 17 profiles were retired on install and its alignments kept under _superseded.

Annotation strings add nothing new to the controlled vocabulary. All citations are model-proposed and live in PMID_claude_generated; none has been read and confirmed by a curator.
