Pestiviridae

A controlled vocabulary stops growing. Free text does not.

A Pestiviridae genome encodes the same 14 modelled proteins as any other, so a curated vocabulary needs exactly 14 names no matter how many genomes you read. Free-text product strings have no such ceiling: each new submitter spells the same proteins a new way. Sampling 158 annotated genomes 100 times over shows the two curves doing exactly that — the module is saturated after 7 genomes, while the source vocabulary is still climbing at 158.

The ratio is the number worth quoting: at the end of each curve, free text costs 20.9 strings per 100 genomes and the controlled vocabulary 8.9 — a 2.4-fold collapse. Reaching 95% of its final size takes the source vocabulary 124 genomes (78% of the set); the module gets there on genome 7.
