murine, feline and porcine leukaemia viruses

Gammaretrovirus holds the leukaemia viruses of mice, cats and pigs, together with the endogenous retroviruses derived from them. Its pol is the reason this module needed a rule the rest did not: Gag and Pol are in the SAME reading frame, separated by a single amber codon that the ribosome reads through.

01What the module calls

Three features on a simple genome, one of which the caller could not see until a stop codon was allowed.

POL carries internal_stop: 1. Gammaretroviral Pol is made by ribosomal read-through of the amber terminating gag, so the best POL profile aligns ACROSS that codon — on Moloney murine leukaemia virus the top-scoring HSP opens 55 residues upstream of the amber and carries exactly one stop, while on feline leukaemia virus it carries none. Without the flag the caller treated the murine match as broken and emitted nothing.

02Where it does not call, and why

Declaring internal_stop lifted POL from 63.2% to 92.1% on the held-out panel, recovering sixteen genomes — Moloney, Friend, Graffi, XMRV and the porcine endogenous retroviruses, every one a complete virus with an intact pol. Four other explanations were tested and discarded first: degenerate endogenous biology, a high bit cutoff, the length window, and an internal-ATG offset. The duplicate-call audit after the change shows POL does not nest inside GAG. Glyco-Gag is a real distinct protein — CUG-initiated and N-terminally extended — but it contains the whole GAG ORF, so declaring it would nest one feature inside another for no gain.

05Method

Coverage is measured by annotating genomes through the real GTO pipeline — annotate_by_viral_pssm-GTO.pl, then the transcript-edit and splice passes, then viral_genome_quality.pl — not by a reimplementation. Spliced features are included, which the flat feature-table path cannot represent. Exemplars are clustered at 95% nucleotide identity over 90% of length, so one row is one distinct sequence rather than one deposit: this module's 262 genomes reduce to 76 exemplars, and without that step a rate would report how often a sequence was deposited rather than how often the module calls it. Call rates are taken over the 69 of 76 exemplars this module actually claimed; an unrouted genome contributes no features and counting it would understate every rate, and this is the same denominator the evaluation and the copy_num decisions used.
