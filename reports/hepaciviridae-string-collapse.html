Hepaciviridae

What the annotation cleanup actually collapsed

Twelve features absorb 159 distinct BV-BRC annotation strings across 218,893 protein occurrences in 63,977 genomes. One genus, so the taxon column is species: 119 of them, though Hepacivirus hominis is 63,146 of the 63,977 genomes and the long tail is where the naming gets inventive. The trap in this family is not spelling. It is that a string reading NS1 means E2 — inherited from the flavivirus naming this family was split out of — and that NS3-4A, NS5AB, NS4 and core-E2 are precursors, not features, so they are excluded rather than binned.
