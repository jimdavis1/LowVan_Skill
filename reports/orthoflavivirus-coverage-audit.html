Orthoflavivirus

Does the flavivirus module actually work?

Dengue, Zika, yellow fever, West Nile, Japanese encephalitis and tick-borne encephalitis are one genus translated as a single polyprotein and cut into fifteen pieces, plus a sixteenth made by a ribosomal frameshift. Every one of those pieces is bounded not by a codon but by where a named enzyme cuts — which makes the boundaries checkable, and made a whole class of error in the source annotation invisible to every rule this kit had.

01What the module covers

One genus, chosen for human-disease burden. Flaviviridae holds 440,150 records but 395,156 are partial — the median Hepacivirus record is a 467-nt NS5B genotyping fragment — so the family was split by genus and this is the first of four.

The polyprotein layouts genuinely differ between genera, which is why one module per genus rather than one per family. Orthoflavivirus contributes 38,735 genomes at ≥9,000 nt against Hepacivirus 5,021, Pestivirus 1,970 and Pegivirus 563.

Six gene symbols — C, E, M, NS1, NS2A, NS5 — already name different proteins in coronaviruses and paramyxoviruses. These are the community names Orthoflavivirus genuinely owns, and renaming them would be worse than the clash. Decided, not overlooked.

Ambiguity. 17.2% of the binned sequences carried at least one ambiguity code. The worst were not marginal: Orthoflavivirus tembusu VN0613 contributed a POLY of 1,261 X, an E of 392 and an NS4A that was 126 X out of ~130 residues. Eleven sequences were kept against the rule because each is the only one its taxon has for that feature and is spoiled by one to four incidental residues — Sokoluk virus NS4A, Dakar bat virus M and NS3, Phnom Penh bat virus NS3/E/NS5/POLY, O. flavi KGH855 NS2A/POLY, Flavivirus sp. M138 POLY at 4 X in 4,013. All eleven are itemised in AMBIGUOUS_KEPT.tsv.

Length. The pipeline had only a floor — 70% of median — and nothing above it, which is why positional halves leaking into POLY needed a hand-written fix and why M, a 75-residue protein, still held sequences up to 500 aa after every other filter. A ceiling at 1.3× median removes 50 sequences with a clean gap either side: the longest legitimate variant is Culex flavivirus ancC at 1.22×, and the junk starts at 3.6×.

208 first-pass profiles plus 129 from the leftover pass. The leftover pass needed a fix to run here at all: it discarded any sequence shorter than a hardcoded 30 residues, so all 60 of 2K's leftovers were dropped and the script reported "0 leftover sequence(s)" for a file holding 60. The floor is now a fraction of the feature's own collection median, and 2K gained the 7 leftover profiles it should always have had.

03Where the boundaries come from

A fifteen-product polyprotein has sixteen termini and not one of them is marked by a codon. Each is where a named enzyme cuts, so each is checkable against published chemistry rather than against the annotation being replaced.

... NS4A ...EPEKQR ↓ SQTDNQLAVFLICVLTLVSAVAA ↓ NEMGWLDK... NS4B ...
                     └──────── 2K, 23 aa ────────┘
        NS2B-NS3 protease                    host signalase
        cuts after the QR                    cuts before the N

From PMID 8445732 (Lin, Amberg, Chambers & Rice, J Virol 1993), PMID 33494395 (Wahaab et al., Pathogens 2021) and PMID 2174669 (Chambers, Hahn, Galler & Rice, Annu Rev Microbiol 1990): NS2B-NS3 requires two basic residues at P2–P1 and a small residue at P1′; the 2K/NS4B site is a host signalase site. So an NS2B-NS3-generated C-terminus ends in R or K, a signalase-generated one ends in a small residue, and an NS2B-NS3-generated N-terminus begins with one.

The check that this is right rather than merely self-consistent. After filtering, 2K measures 23 residues at p10, median and p90. The filter knows only residue classes at the two ends and is never told a length — it reproduces Lin 1993's experimental result, that the NS4A/2K site sits exactly 23 residues upstream of the signalase site, from sequence alone.

min_len and max_len are the observed range of each cleaned collection rather than a hand-drawn window. The previous values came from the contaminated distributions and were wide enough to admit that 18-residue helicase fragment as 2K.

2K is called on 94.8% of well-sequenced genomes. A 23-residue signal peptide with 17 profiles, whose collection was 13.6% contaminated before this build and which had no leftover profiles at all. It is now mid-table rather than the weakest feature.

That is the figure comparable to other modules in this series — Alphaflexiviridae 92.4%, Tobamovirus 89.4%, Hepeviridae 89.1% — and it is the right comparison because those modules declare four to six features each, where this one declares fifteen and marks twelve of them essential.

pr is the one feature whose declared bound was wrong, and the module found it. BV-BRC annotates no pr peptide for the tick-borne clade at all: all 632 training sequences are mosquito-borne, running 69–97 aa with 500 of them at exactly 91. The profiles nonetheless call pr correctly in tick-borne genomes at its true, shorter boundary — TBE 244 calls at median 59, Kyasanur Forest 164 at 59, Omsk 51 at 59, louping ill 30 at 59, each ≥99% below the collection minimum. Tick-borne prM is shorter than mosquito-borne prM, so their pr genuinely is ~59 aa. A min_len of 69 flagged a correct call across an entire clade; it is now 55. The lesson is that bounds must reflect what a feature is across the taxon, not what the training collection happens to contain.

Every constrained terminus now agrees with its enzyme on 100.0% of sequences in all fifteen collections. Before filtering that agreement ran 84–98%, worst at 2K (84.4% N-terminal, 87.5% C-terminal) and mature C (87.0% / 84.8%).

07What it cannot do

The part that matters. None of the following is fixed by a threshold.

NS1′ is called, and it cannot distinguish the JEV vaccine lineage from its wild parent. This is the limit of sequence-based identification for this feature, not a gap in the reference set.

NS1′ is built and shipped as a transcript_edit feature: 104 hand-curated nucleotide references, each carrying the slipped base duplicated, from which the annotator fills a one-base gap in the genome and translates. Every one of the 104 donor genomes recovers its own protein byte-identically, and no false positive appears outside the serogroup — 0 of 72 dengue, Zika and yellow fever genomes get a call or even a partial_cds.

Why no threshold fixes this. Every one of those A66 genomes carries the slippery heptanucleotide at phase 2 of NS2A codon 6, intact and in the correct frame — 70 of 79 readable JEV genomes do. What disables NS1′ in the vaccine lineage is a single substitution 60 bases further on, in the pseudoknot that stimulates the slip. A sequence match sees a near-perfect reference and fills the gap; the structure that decides whether the ribosome actually slips is not something the match can read. Checking the heptamer before accepting a correction — the obvious guard — would catch none of them.

What is and is not affected. Wild-type JEV, West Nile, Usutu, Murray Valley and Cacipacore all make NS1′ and are called correctly. The error is confined to the attenuated SA14-14-2 lineage and to six other JEV genomes carrying the same allele, whose status — unlabelled vaccine derivative or genuine A66 field isolate — cannot be settled from sequence. Treat an NS1′ call on a strain named as a vaccine as unreliable.

The reference set under-covers JEV. 97 of the 104 references are West Nile and 4 are JEV, because that is the ratio in which BV-BRC annotates the feature. Seven wild-type JEV genomes were therefore demoted to Uncorrected NS1-prime protein encoding region with no translation rather than corrected. That is the mechanism degrading gracefully, but JEV coverage is thin and a denser JEV reference set is the one improvement that would help.

POLY fragments on poorly sequenced assemblies. 421 genomes receive between 2 and 7 separate POLY calls instead of one. Every fragment comes from the same profile, so this is not a coverage gap: those genomes carry a median 7.9% ambiguous bases (p90 29.5%) against 0.0000% in the 3,450 single-call genomes, and 96% of them exceed 1%. The fragment boundaries sit on the N-runs — one Zika genome is called at 56+5196, then 5717+690, leaving a 465-nt gap. The annotator aligns what is sequenced and cannot bridge runs of Ns. This is assembly quality, not the module, but it is the largest single contributor to the headline quality figure.

2K's profiles recall only 61.3% of their own training set. Of its 191 sequences, the 17 profiles call 117 back within the declared length range. Every other feature sits at 98.6–100% — NS1, NS3, NS5, prM, POLY and E at exactly 100%. A 23-residue profile at a bit cutoff of 25 has very little signal to work with, and this is the honest limit of modelling a signal peptide with a PSSM at all. It is called on 94.8% of well-sequenced genomes in practice, so the profiles generalise better than they self-recall — but the margin is thin and a future rebuild of this feature should expect to need more profiles, not a lower cutoff.

Mature C is the weakest feature at 91.8%, and ancC at 97.1%. The two overlap in length and the source names both "capsid protein", so 108 ancC records had to be moved out of C by homology. A residual 8.2% of genomes get no C call.

1,309 records were discarded and cannot be recovered. Of the 1,422 whose termini failed, homology could place only 113. The rest break down as 1,094 whose best match is their own feature — the right protein with broken ends, which has no bin to move to and would need its boundary guessed — 188 matching nothing at ≥80% coverage against 55,119 clean sequences, and 27 matching another feature but failing that feature's own junction rule. Trimming was considered and rejected: a trimmed boundary is a guess, where the sequences that pass are evidence.

219 annotation strings remain unbinned, holding 2,826 features — 0.6% of the taxon. They are listed in UNASSIGNED_TRACKING.tsv and are mostly "hypothetical protein" and bare ORF numbers that name no protein.

No citation here has been read by a curator. All references live in PMID_claude_generated and the curator-reviewed PMID field is empty for every feature. The three cleavage-chemistry citations were retrieved and their identifiers verified against Europe PMC, but retrieval is not review.

These figures cover the fifteen PSSM features. NS1′ is reported in §05. It had to be measured by a separate invocation because run_gto_eval.py ran the annotator and the quality scorer with nothing in between, so transcript-edited and spliced features were never called during evaluation — for any module. That is now fixed: the evaluator calls them before scoring, so a module's declared features and its measured features are the same set. Audits published before this fix understate their modules, Togaviridae TF and the coronavirus ORF1ab/NSP12 among them.

Measured by rarefaction over the 3,624 genomes annotated by both, 100 replicates. The source vocabulary reaches 95% of its final size only at 2,911 genomes — 80% of the set — because it keeps accumulating new spellings with every genome added. The module's reaches 95% at a single genome, because a controlled vocabulary is closed by construction. 9.8 source strings per 100 genomes against 0.4.

All fifteen strings were reused from the existing controlled set. Two were reused rather than invented — Nucleocapsid protein and RNA-dependent RNA polymerase, both already used by Togaviridae for mature peptides. 2K reads "Signal peptide of NS4B" because that is literally what it is. check_annotations.py reports one dissent: it wants L, NSP12, nsP4 or p90 for the RdRp rather than NS5. The symbol was kept on the same standing that justifies NSP12 for coronaviruses — a large literature in major human pathogens and an active drug target.
