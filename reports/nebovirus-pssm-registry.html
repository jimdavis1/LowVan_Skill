Newbury agent-1 and the bovine enteric caliciviruses. Two ORFs, the Sapovirus/Lagovirus layout: ORF1 carries the non-structural proteins and the capsid in frame, ORF2 the minor structural protein. These genomes were annotated with nothing before this module existed — 34 near-complete Nebovirus genomes run through the four shipped Caliciviridae modules produced zero features of any kind.

What was missed, and why

NS6 — Its motif is deliberately left at 24 of 26. Every widening tried matched a second site inside the window, which would place the boundary wrongly rather than leave it absent, so two polyproteins are skipped instead.

POLY — 26 of the 221 unique sequences in the dump are full-length polyproteins. The rest of the deposited population is fragments — 161 of 349 features are annotated RNA-dependent RNA polymerase at a median 167 residues against a true 485.
