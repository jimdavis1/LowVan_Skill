Crinivirus

What the annotation cleanup actually collapsed

Crinivirus is bipartite and its accessory proteins are named by mass — and the masses do not correspond to proteins. p22 is four non-homologous groups at 188–193 aa; p59 and p60 are one group of 78 that is the HSP90 homolog; p26/p27/p28 are one diverged family and p8/p9 another, each shipped as a single feature.
