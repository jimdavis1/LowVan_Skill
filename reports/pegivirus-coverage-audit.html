01A separate module, on genome organisation

Pegivirus sits next to Pestivirus and Hepacivirus in the old Flaviviridae, and its polyprotein is cut differently enough that it cannot share a module with either.

Pestivirus   Npro - C - Erns - E1 - E2 - p7 - NS2 - NS3 - NS4A-4B-5A-5B
Pegivirus            (no core)  E1 - E2 -   p7-NS2  - NS3 - NS4A-4B-5A-5B

Declaring them together would report Npro, Erns and C as missing on every one of 7,340 pegivirus records. A module is a group of taxa that share a genome organisation, not a clade.

02No capsid, and no separate p7

Both absences are in the literature and both are visible in the data.

No core feature is declared. GBV-A and GBV-C are unique in the family for lacking a well-defined capsid protein. The dump holds 9 records under a core or capsid string and not one is a pegivirus capsid: three are 15-22 aa signal peptides (MELLWLFSVFQVAGG, MAVLLLLLVAEAGAILAPATHA) and one 183-aa record is hepatitis B virus core protein (MDIDPYKEFGATVELLSFLPSDFF...), misfiled.

No p7 feature either. The canonical product list is E1, E2, NS2, NS3, NS4A, NS4B, NS5A, NS5B — p7 is not a separately recognised product. The dump agrees: p7-NS2 fused is the dominant annotation at 190 records tight on 253 aa, against 40 for NS2 alone at 238 and 9 for p7 alone at 68.

The three annotations agree on where NS2 ends — all share a C-terminal Thr — and contradict each other on where it starts: 253 − 238 = 15, not the 68 a separate p7 would require. The internal boundary is not placed, because the data does not support placing it. NS2 is built from the p7-NS2 and NS2 records together.

NS5A at 66.9% is the outlier, and it is the source-annotation ceiling again. Of the 17 taxa that miss it, not one has a single NS5A training sequence. The leftover pool is 7 sequences, so re-clustering cannot help. E1 at 76.2% is the same shape.

Zero genomes called nothing — every routed genome gets at least the polyprotein — which is the strongest single result here and the one Pestiviridae does not match (13 of 172).

07What it cannot do

The junction chemistry has not been checked against published cleavage sites. The JUNCTION table for this module is deliberately almost empty — only the polyprotein's initiator Met — because imposing HCV's residue classes on pegivirus would be an assumption, and a filter that encodes an assumption will later appear to confirm it. Pestiviridae's table was measured first and linked to citations; this one has not been, so no constraint is applied. That measurement is the obvious next improvement.

20.8% genuinely clean on a species-capped panel that gives 46 species equal weight. NS5A and E1 account for most of the shortfall and neither is fixable from sequence.
