01What the module covers

One circular genome of about 8 kb, read in overlapping frames.

Deltapapillomavirus is the fibropapillomavirus genus of ungulates — bovine papillomavirus 1 and 2 and their relatives — and the only papillomaviruses that transform fibroblasts as well as epithelium. The module is built on genomes from cattle, with camel, deer, giraffe and roe deer types.

It declares 10 features: E1, E1E4, E2, E5, E6, E7, E8E2, L1, L2, L2_MAT. E5 is the principal transforming protein of this genus, not an accessory: BPV1 E5 is a 44-residue membrane peptide that activates the PDGF receptor. The module models it as a feature in its own right.

07What it cannot do

The limits, each measured rather than supposed.

E5 is called on most but not all of the genus.

Every mature L2 call in this module begins immediately after a furin site in its own L2 precursor, which is the chemical invariant rather than a fixed offset: furin cleaves after R-X-[KR]-R. Across the family 648 of 681 calls on ambiguity-clean genomes cut after a full site (95.2%).

Use this module on complete Deltapapillomavirus genomes. It calls 7 core features on essentially every genome of the genus; the spliced features are called when the junction is present and readable, and their rate is stated above rather than tuned.
