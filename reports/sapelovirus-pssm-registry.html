What was missed, and why

The leader is the one feature the source barely annotates: 11 records in a genus of 435 genomes. It is now built from leaders derived as the polyprotein N-terminus up to the start of VP4, a boundary that falls on a canonical 3C Q-G cleavage site in 166 of 170 genomes where both the polyprotein and VP4 are placed.
