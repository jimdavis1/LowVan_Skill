Erythroparvovirus

6 of 6 features built · 15 PSSMs · 100% callable

B19 VP1 is 781 aa and VP2 is 554 aa — VP2 is VP1 without the 227-residue VP1u (PMID 11799199; RefSeq NC_000883 agrees, but the literature is what settles it). Forty unique deposits named “VP2” were 781 aa, i.e. VP1 under the wrong name. That alone would have been survivable; what made it fatal is that fasta-cluster-pssm drops any cluster whose consensus is a substring of another’s, and the real 554 aa VP2 is a substring of VP1. Both genuine VP2 clusters were discarded and VP2 shipped as a VP1 profile, which then called VP2 at VP1’s exact coordinates in 281 of 300 genomes while self-recall, call rate and the annotation string all looked correct.

What was missed, and why

Five further deposits named VP2 at 602–617 aa carry VP1’s own MSKESGKWWES… start rather than VP2’s: truncated VP1, not an alternative start. Length cannot separate those from a real VP2, since 602 is close to 554; only the N-terminus shows it.
