EIAV is the lentivirus of horses and the smallest module in this family — one virus, little vocabulary, and the clearest example in the set of a tiny accessory gene that is nonetheless essential. Four features carry profiles; rev is declared and called by the splice caller, and tat is recognised but not declared.

01What the module calls

Four profiled features, and an 11-of-11 accessory gene smaller than most peptides.

S2 is 59–83 aa and is called on every exemplar. It was marked essential on evidence: 30 of 30 held-out genomes, and out of frame with env in 29 of 29 — a separate gene, not a cleavage product of the envelope it sits inside.

02Where it does not call, and why

tat is spliced in EIAV and is not declared; the string is recognised and recorded, which is why the collapse report shows it bound to a target the module JSON does not carry. gag, pol and env are each called on 10 of 11 exemplars; the one miss is a single short record, not a profile failure.

The remainder are recorded in UNASSIGNED and DISCARDED: the first is vocabulary no rule reached, the second is records the source itself calls partial or truncated, which the project discards rather than trains on.

04Vocabulary saturation

How fast each vocabulary stops growing as genomes are added.

The deposited vocabulary keeps growing; the module's does not. At the depth both reach, 23.7 distinct source strings correspond to 5.0 controlled annotations, a 4.7-fold collapse. The module reaches 95% of its final vocabulary after 12 proteins against the source's 62.

05Method

Coverage is measured by annotating de-replicated exemplars through the real GTO pipeline — annotate_by_viral_pssm-GTO.pl, then the transcript-edit and splice passes, then viral_genome_quality.pl — not by a reimplementation. Spliced features are included, which the flat feature-table path cannot represent. Rates are per exemplar, and exemplars are clustered at 95% nucleotide identity, so one row is one distinct sequence rather than one deposit.

Measured 27 October 2026 from the shipped module: the EIAV block of Viral_PSSM.json, its profiles and collections, and the 27 October coverage re-annotation over 11 exemplars de-replicated from 265 genomes. Vocabulary figures come from collections/synonyms.tsv, written by the module's own builder from the naming rules, and count deposited features rather than unique sequences.
