Nimanivirus

Macrobrachium rosenbergii Golda virus, the ronivirus of the giant freshwater prawn and the largest genome in its family at 28.9–29.1 kb. Four features. It is a separate module from Okavirus because it encodes a different protein set — and because the two glycoprotein precursors cannot be aligned at all.

02How it was built

A tidy vocabulary, and one measurement that settled the module boundary.

Seven distinct strings collapse to four controlled annotations. The module's contribution here is not collapse but interpretation — binding four positional ORF labels to what the proteins are.

The module split was measured, not assumed. The ICTV profile records that MrGV lacks the triple-membrane-spanning p25 of the okavirus precursor and lacks ORFX, which already separates the protein sets. That is a claim about alignments, so it was re-measured: MrGV ORF3 (1503 aa) blastp'd against YHV pp3 (1666 aa) returns no meaningful alignment at all — best HSP 20.4 bits, 23.7% identity over 38 residues, which is noise. The two precursors are not alignable, so no okavirus cleavage site can be transferred and no PSSM could serve both.

The slippery heptamer was read off the genomes. This genus uses GGGTTTT where Okavirus uses AAATTTT. In MW590703 the measured site begins at nt 14925 — exactly the coordinate PMID 35011135 publishes (5'-G14925GGUUUU14931-3').

07What it cannot do

One named gap, and it is the important one.

ORF3 ships whole, and no cleavage product is declared. ICTV asserts that this precursor encodes homologues of gp116 and gp64 behind an N-terminal signal peptide, with three predicted transmembrane domains. But no cleavage site is published for MrGV, and the okavirus sites cannot be transferred — the precursors are not alignable (§02). Cutting here would mean inventing the coordinates, so the products are recorded as a gap rather than guessed at. This is the single largest thing the module does not do.

Only three frameshift references could be built. Just one genome deposits pp1ab; two more were assembled from pp1a + ORF1b pairs, and 19 further genomes were refused for ambiguous bases. All three references are 7797 aa.

ORF1b is not modelled — 11 deposits at 2776 aa. ORF1b is never translated on its own, being reached only through the frameshift.

ORF4 is not declared. The ICTV profile does not state whether MrGV has one, and no deposit exists.

The panel is one species. Nimanivirus lahi is the only species in the genus and its two variants are 97.8% identical, so the 30-genome panel measures consistency rather than breadth. A genuinely divergent nimanivirus would be a new test this module has not had.

08What the run found

One measurement that changed the shape of the build.

The two ronivirus genera needed different slippery heptamers. Had the ICTV family-level wording been taken as a single constraint, the frameshift reference set would have covered one genus and silently missed the other — the same failure shape recorded for Nodaviridae, where a single assumed heptamer covered half a module. Reading the 7 nt before the pp1a stop in each genome is cheap and settles it.

Ambiguity codes, not the module, cap PP1AB. Measuring the call rate against IUPAC content turned an apparently poor 6.7% into a fully explained figure, and prevented a pointless attempt to tune the reference set.

At equal depth over 116 proteins the source and the module each carry 5 distinct strings, a ratio of 1.0×. The source vocabulary here was already tidy, so the gain is interpretive rather than numerical: ORF1a, ORF2 and ORF3 become a replicase polyprotein, a nucleocapsid and a glycoprotein precursor, and ORF1b is deliberately not called at all.
