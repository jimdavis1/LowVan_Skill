01What the module covers

One circular genome of about 8 kb, read in overlapping frames.

Betapapillomavirus is the cutaneous human genus associated with epidermodysplasia verruciformis and with the commensal flora of normal skin. The genome is a single circular double-stranded DNA molecule of roughly 7.5 kb. The module is built on genomes from humans, with two macaque types and one bandicoot deposit.

07What it cannot do

The limits, each measured rather than supposed.

The spliced features are called on about half the genomes.

Every mature L2 call in this module begins immediately after a furin site in its own L2 precursor, which is the chemical invariant rather than a fixed offset: furin cleaves after R-X-[KR]-R. Across the family 648 of 681 calls on ambiguity-clean genomes cut after a full site (95.2%).

Use this module on complete Betapapillomavirus genomes. It calls 7 core features on essentially every genome of the genus; the spliced features are called when the junction is present and readable, and their rate is stated above rather than tuned.
