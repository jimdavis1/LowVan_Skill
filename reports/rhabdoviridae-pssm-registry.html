Separately, G_MAT and G_SP are coordinate operations on curated G rather than binning products, and both now work. G_MAT gives 505 columns at 1000+ bits. G_SP was previously abandoned on a measurement error: a 19-residue profile scored with psiblast against proteins carries composition-based statistics that halve it (22 bits, read as “impossible”), where the annotator’s actual tblastn against the genome gives 47. Rebuilt as ten per-cluster profiles — 39–46 bits median, 100% self-recall — because a signal-peptide profile does not transfer even between lyssaviruses. Rabies now yields the full tiling: G 3318–4892, SP 3318–3374, mature 3375–4889.
