01What the module covers

One circular genome of about 8 kb, read in overlapping frames.

Taupapillomavirus is a carnivore genus, Felis catus papillomavirus 2 and its relatives. The genome is a single circular double-stranded DNA molecule. The module is built on genomes from cats and dogs, with ferret and seal types.

07What it cannot do

The limits, each measured rather than supposed.

A few deposits carry an E5 string the module does not model.

Every mature L2 call in this module begins immediately after a furin site in its own L2 precursor, which is the chemical invariant rather than a fixed offset: furin cleaves after R-X-[KR]-R. Across the family 648 of 681 calls on ambiguity-clean genomes cut after a full site (95.2%).

Use this module on complete Taupapillomavirus genomes. It calls 7 core features on essentially every genome of the genus; the spliced features are called when the junction is present and readable, and their rate is stated above rather than tuned.
