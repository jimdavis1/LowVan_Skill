Tymovirus

What the annotation cleanup actually collapsed

Three overlapping ORFs and a vocabulary that includes two outright misspellings — RNA-dependend RNA polymerase and RNA-depedent RNA polymerase — alongside positional labels (TYMVgp1/2/3) and length-encoded ones (unnamed protein product; 206K ORF (AA 1-1844)). None of the positional labels is trusted as a name; each is admitted only inside its feature's length window.
