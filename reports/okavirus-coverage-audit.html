Okavirus

Gill-associated virus and yellow head virus, the shrimp roniviruses — invertebrate nidoviruses with a gene order found nowhere else in the order: the nucleoprotein gene sits upstream of the glycoprotein gene, and there is no discrete M gene. Nine features, including three cleavage products of one precursor and an 86 aa protein in an overlapping frame that no database holds.

02How it was built

Rules over the annotation strings, then two derivations and one extraction.

The ORF1a product appears as replicase polyprotein 1a, ORF1a polyprotein pp1a and ORF1a; the ORF3 precursor as structural glycoprotein, polyprotein pp3, spike glycoprotein, envelope structural glycoprotein, polyprotein precursor and mutant envelope glycoprotein. 21 distinct strings collapse to 5 controlled annotations, 4.2×.

The three ORF3 products are derived, not binned. The deposits named gp116 and gp64 are 267–338 aa fragments of a true 899 aa and 539 aa protein. PMID 12655087 placed the cuts between Ala228/Thr229 and Ala1127/Leu1128 of the 1666 aa precursor, and both verify exactly against every 1666 aa deposit here:

site 1   ...LCFFITPAFA | TILSGI...     A-F-A, cut after A228
site 2   ...SIISPASA   | LAPRQ...      A-S-A, cut after A1127

Both are the [Ala-X-Ala] signal-peptidase-type-1 motif the ICTV profile describes. The cut is made on the motif, never on the residue number. A 162 nt indel in ORF3 separates the YHV subtypes, so the second site sits at 1101 in GAV, 1104 in GAV genotype 3, 1117 in YHV-8 and 1127 in YHV-1 — a column number would mis-cut every genotype but one. The derivation refuses any precursor where a site does not match exactly once; 9 of 11 were cut.

06Are the calls right

The tiling test is the gate for a multi-product precursor.

Summing a precursor's products across every panel genome and subtracting the precursor should give about zero. Here:

1 of 6 (YHV-8):  +1, a one-codon overlap at the p25|gp116 junction

07What it cannot do

The limits, named.

No mature peptides are declared for the replicase. The ICTV profile states that the number and composition of the nsps released from pp1ab “is not yet known”. The dump holds deposits annotated 3C-like protease (335–343 aa), helicase (660–846 aa) and RNA polymerase (92–286 aa), but these are domain fragments with no published junctions. Only the 3CLpro specificity VxHE↓(L/V) is characterised (PMID 12502857), and a specificity is not a coordinate. Declaring a mature peptide would mean inventing its boundaries.

ORF1b is not modelled. ORF1b is never translated on its own, being reached only through the frameshift as part of pp1ab. Deposits under that name are a submitter convention for the open reading frame, not a protein the virus makes.

It calls nothing from an ORF1b amplicon. Of 17 whole-taxon exemplars, 6 called nothing; all six cover only ORF1b or the ORF1a/ORF1b junction. KX883632 (5040 nt, GAV) is the instructive case: it routes at 6216 bits and every profile returns stop-free 0, because the contig spans the frameshift and no single reading frame is open across it.

ORF4 carries no copy_num. It is genus-partial by construction — 83 aa in GAV, truncated after 20 codons in YHV — so marking it essential would flag every yellow head virus genome.

The cleavage products carry no copy_num either. apply_copy_num.py blocked P25 and GP116 as lying wholly inside PP3 and proposed GP64, which scores a non-zero standalone rate only because it runs to the precursor's own stop. Marking one of three products of one precursor essential and not the other two would assert a difference that does not exist; PP3 carries the essentiality.

A source disagreement is recorded, not resolved. The ICTV profile calls the N-terminal product p25 and the 2008 Walker & Sittidilokratna review calls it p22, both at 228 aa; 228 aa is about 25 kDa, so p25 is used. ICTV gives ORF4 as 98 aa and the review as 83 aa; both GAV deposits here are 83 aa, so 83 is used.

08What the run found

Two published coordinates corrected by measurement.

ORFX does not start where the ICTV profile says. The profile states it “commences 3 nucleotides downstream of the pp3 initiation codon”. A +3 nt offset is the same reading frame as pp3 and cannot encode a different protein. Measured directly in all three reference genomes, the start is +5 nt, in the +2 frame:

YHV  NC_043505   pp3 starts nt 20985   ORFX nt 20990 (+5)  86 aa
GAV  NC_010306   pp3 starts nt 20675   ORFX nt 20680 (+5)  86 aa
YHV8 KX947267    pp3 starts nt 20979   ORFX nt 20984 (+5)  86 aa

15 extractions across the genus are all exactly 86 aa — the length the ICTV profile itself gives for px.

The slippery heptamer differs between the two ronivirus genera. The ICTV profile gives the family's site as “AAAUUUU or GGGUUUU”. Read off the genomes at the pp1a/pp1b junction, Okavirus uses AAATTTT in GAV, YHV-1 and YHV-8, while Nimanivirus uses GGGTTTT. A single assumed heptamer would silently have covered half the work.

A kit defect was found and fixed. check_literature_gaps.py documents a RECOGNISED verdict for a string bound to NOT_MODELLED — a deliberate decision — but collection_engine.py wrote UNASSIGNED for those strings, so the verdict was unreachable and every deliberate decision came back as a defect in the rules. Found on this module's nsp fragments. Fixed in both the engine and the collapse statistic; the binned figure is unchanged at 84.3%, confirming the fix is behaviour-preserving.

09What the vocabulary bought

Seventeen source strings to nine, measured at equal depth.

Rarefied over 16 genomes carrying at least 5 kb of sequence and at least one deposited protein, source and module compared at the depth both reach:

at 41 proteins:   source 17.0 distinct strings
                  module   9.0 distinct strings      1.9x

The six-genome held-out panel was too small to rarefy — 12 proteins against a floor of 40 — so the pool was widened to every okavirus genome carrying real multi-protein content. That is legitimate here because a vocabulary comparison asks what the two naming schemes are, not how well the module recalls unseen genomes, and so needs no hold-out. The recall figures in §05 remain the held-out ones.

Four further strings — 3C-like protease, helicase, RNA polymerase and ORF1b — are bound to NOT_MODELLED as deliberate decisions rather than left unbound, and are reported as RECOGNISED by the literature gap check.
