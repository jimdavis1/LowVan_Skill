Betaflexiviridae_MP is the half of the family that moves by a single movement protein rather than a triple gene block: Trichovirus, Vitivirus, Citrivirus, Tepovirus, Prunevirus, Chordovirus, Divavirus, Wamavirus. Between them they cause apple chlorotic leaf spot, grapevine Pinot gris disease, citrus leaf blotch and cherry mottle leaf. The two accessories are absent by biology rather than by failure: NABP is called on 92% of Vitivirus and 38% of Prunevirus and in none of Citrivirus, Chordovirus, Divavirus, Tepovirus or Wamavirus, and VITI_ORF2 is called on 84% of Vitivirus and nowhere else at all. Reporting those as 46% and 38% of the module would describe the taxonomy, not the module.
