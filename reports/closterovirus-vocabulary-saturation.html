Closterovirus

A controlled vocabulary stops growing. Free text does not.

A Closterovirus genome encodes the same 14 modelled proteins as any other, so a curated vocabulary needs exactly 14 names no matter how many genomes you read. Free-text product strings have no such ceiling: each new submitter spells the same proteins a new way. Sampling 149 annotated genomes 100 times over shows the two curves doing exactly that — the module is saturated after 54 genomes, while the source vocabulary is still climbing at 149.
