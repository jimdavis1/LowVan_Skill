Chicken calicivirus. Two ORFs in the Sapovirus/Lagovirus/Nebovirus layout: ORF1 carries the non-structural proteins and the capsid in frame, ORF2 the minor structural protein. These genomes were annotated with nothing before this module existed. The dump labels every record Chicken calicivirus or genus Bavovirus, but it holds two distinct viruses that share this layout and little else.

What was missed, and why

POLY — The cutoff of 4,075 bits is high because both clades are tightly sampled: 24 full-length polyproteins score 4,313-4,780 and nothing else in the family scores above 1,817. It sits in that gap, so a genuinely novel bavovirus lineage below roughly 90% identity would not be called.

VP2 — Six of the 21 VP2 sequences are the divergent 215 aa form, and all six come from a single metagenomic survey. The clade is real but thinly sampled.
