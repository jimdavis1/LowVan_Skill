Orthoflavivirus

A controlled vocabulary stops growing. Free text does not.

Every rubivirus encodes the same seven proteins, so a curated vocabulary should need exactly seven names no matter how many genomes you read. Free-text product strings have no such ceiling: each new submitter spells the same proteins a new way. Sampling 3624 annotated genomes 100 times over shows the two curves doing exactly that — the module is saturated after one genome, while the source vocabulary is still climbing at 3624.
