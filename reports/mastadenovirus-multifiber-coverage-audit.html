Does the Mastadenovirus, multi-fiber genomes module work?

Human adenovirus F and its simian relatives carry two or more fiber genes and are kept apart from the one-fiber mastadenoviruses. Core calling is high; most poor genomes here are partial or carry interrupted genes, which the checked examples confirm is the sequence and not the profile.

01What the module covers

Mastadenoviruses that carry more than one fiber gene: human adenovirus F (types 40 and 41) and the simian and rhesus adenoviruses whose fiber census places them here.

Human adenovirus 41 with its two fiber genes, the long and short fibers that put it in this module.

Genomes were assigned to modules from sequence alone: the genus by best hexon match, and one-fiber versus multi-fiber mastadenoviruses by a census of intact fiber genes classed by literature orthology (Podgorski 2023, PMID 36610683). Deposited names were never used to assign a genome.

02How it was built

Membership by homology to curated seed collections; the deposited name is recorded but never decides.

special featuresE1A_12S, E1A_13S, E4ORF67, K33

Paralogue families were split into orthogroup features by genome position, and each is called by sequence, never by location. Features with no literature function are named “Uncharacterized lineage-specific protein”. Splice junctions come from the literature and were extended to other lineages by aligning donor and acceptor sites; every spliced product was translated and checked in frame.

Self-recall (each feature’s profiles searched against its own collection) is 100% for nearly every feature; the registry page lists every feature.

Essential genes (copy_num) follow the curator’s rules: never on uncharacterized proteins, special features or lineage-specific genes, and excluded wherever a cutoff, copy-number or coverage problem is on record. Essential here: DBP, E1B55K, E3_14_7K, E4ORF3, IVA2, I_LEADER, K22, PIX, PROTEASE, PV, PVI, TP_MAT, VIII_C.

The subset deliberately over-samples unusual genomes, so its quality rate is a floor for the module, not an estimate of it. Poor genomes are dominated by sequence problems: deposits with no cytosine, N gaps, partial genomes and genes interrupted by stop codons.
