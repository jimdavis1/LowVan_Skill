MammalianOrthoreovirus

11 of 11 features built · 31 PSSMs · 89% callable

Mammalian orthoreovirus (MRV). Ten double-stranded RNA segments: three large (L1–L3, ~3.9 kb), three medium (M1–M3, ~2.2 kb) and four small (S1–S4, ~1.2–1.4 kb). S1 is bicistronic, encoding the cell-attachment protein sigma-1 and the non-structural sigma-1s from overlapping frames, so eleven proteins come from ten segments.
