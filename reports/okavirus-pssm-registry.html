What was missed, and why

No mature peptides are declared for the replicase. The ICTV profile states that the number and composition of the nsps released from pp1ab “is not yet known”. The dump does hold deposits annotated 3C-like protease (335–343 aa), helicase (660–846 aa) and RNA polymerase (92–286 aa), but these are domain fragments with no published junctions; only the 3CLpro specificity VxHE↓(L/V) is characterised (PMID 12502857), and a specificity is not a coordinate. They are bound to NOT_MODELLED as a deliberate decision. ORF1b deposits are likewise not modelled: ORF1b is never translated on its own, being reached only through the frameshift.

The three ORF3 products are derived by cutting the precursor at two measured sites, not binned from deposits — the deposits named gp116 and gp64 are 267–338 aa fragments of a true 899 aa and 539 aa protein. PMID 12655087 placed the cuts between Ala228/Thr229 and Ala1127/Leu1128 of the 1666 aa precursor, and both verify exactly against every 1666 aa deposit here: ...LCFFITPAFA | TILSGI... and ...SIISPASA | LAPRQ..., each an [Ala-X-Ala] signal-peptidase-type-1 motif with the cut after the second Ala. The cut is made on the motif, never on the residue number: a 162 nt indel in ORF3 separates the YHV subtypes, so the second site sits at 1101 in GAV, 1117 in YHV-8 and 1127 in YHV-1. Over nine precursors, p25 came out at exactly 228 aa in all nine and the three products tile the precursor with a sum-minus-precursor of zero.
