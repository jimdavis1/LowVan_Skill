What was missed, and why

The ICTV profile records that MrGV lacks the triple-membrane-spanning p25 protein of the okavirus precursor and lacks ORFX. Both are probed for here and come back ABSENT, which is the required result: it confirms that no okavirus rule has leaked across into a genus that does not use it. ORF1b deposits — 11 of them, at 2776 aa — are not modelled, because ORF1b is never translated on its own.

No mature peptides are declared, and that is a documented limit rather than an oversight. ICTV asserts that this precursor encodes homologues of gp116 and gp64 behind an N-terminal signal peptide. But no cleavage site is published for MrGV, and the okavirus sites cannot be transferred: measured here, MrGV ORF3 against YHV pp3 returns no meaningful alignment at all — the best HSP is 20.4 bits at 23.7% identity over 38 residues, which is noise. The precursor therefore ships whole, and the products are a named gap rather than an invented cut.
