01What the module covers

One genus, two nested protein sets, and the most heavily deposited taxon in the family.

Dependoparvovirus is the adeno-associated viruses and their relatives. The genome carries two genes and eight declared features, because both genes are read from more than one start.

The replication gene gives Rep78, Rep68, Rep52 and Rep40; the capsid gene gives VP1, VP2 and VP3. AAP, the assembly-activating protein, is read from a third frame inside the capsid gene. Rep78, VP1 and AAP carry copy_num and are the three the quality score requires.

The nested forms are exempt from the consolidation rule that would otherwise merge a profile whose consensus is a substring of another's. Applied globally that rule emptied REP68 entirely; it is now applied per module, with the nested sets named and excluded.

Routing was measured against the 25 reference contigs this module ships, of which 13 were used by the exemplar set.

All 54 exemplars routed. Fifty-two routed to this module, one to Hamaparvovirinae and one to Protoparvovirus. The two that went elsewhere are divergent isolates with no close relative among the 25 reference contigs; routing is nucleotide identity, so a genome whose nearest neighbour sits in another module goes there.

Nothing was left unrouted, which is the better of the two failure modes: a misrouted genome is annotated with the wrong module's features and that is visible, while an unrouted one simply produces nothing.

07What it cannot do

Four limits, each measured rather than supposed.

AAP does not start at an ATG, and the module now says so. All 34 of 34 deposited AAP sequences begin with methionine; before this fix 1 of 169 calls did, the rest beginning at isoleucine, serine, leucine or arginine, and 75 running past the declared maximum length. AAP is translated from a nested alternative reading frame of cap starting at a non-canonical CUG codon (PMID 20479244, PMID 39205189), so the caller's habit of scanning upstream for a methionine had nothing to find and walked past the real start. Two settings fix two different halves of that. Clearing upstream_ext stops the scan, and the call then lands on the CUG: the modal length becomes 204 residues, matching the deposits, and nothing exceeds the window. Setting start_to_met renders that initiator as M rather than the leucine an ordinary CUG would give.

The N-terminus is improved, not settled, and the methionine is partly a convention. 57 of 169 calls now sit at exactly 204 residues, the deposits' modal length, against none before; the median moved from 209 to 203 and the longest call from 262 to 214. For the other 112 the start is still approximate, and start_to_met writes an M on whatever residue the call begins at. So “169 of 169 begin with methionine” is true by construction and is not evidence that the start is right. Read an AAP call as a protein of about the correct size whose N-terminus is exact in roughly a third of genomes. AAP remains marked essential — it cleared the 0.85 called-over-routed bar — so the genomes without it are flagged.

No splice features are declared. Rep68 and Rep40 are handled as PSSM features built from their own deposits, not through the splice caller. That works because they are deposited often enough to cluster, and it means the module calls the protein without modelling the junction that makes it.

The genus is dominated by engineered genomes. Recombinant AAV vector constructs are deposited under Dependoparvovirus in large numbers. They route and then call nothing, because the vector keeps the inverted terminal repeats and replaces everything between them. They inflate the denominator of any call-rate figure computed over the whole dump rather than over the sampled panel.

Use this module for AAV genomes. AAP's length window is settled and its start codon is understood, but its exact N-terminus is right in about a third of genomes and the leading methionine is supplied by the caller, so do not treat an AAP start as determined sequence. Do not compute call rates over the whole deposited genus without first removing the vector constructs.

08What the run found

What the build turned up that was not expected.

The consolidation rule nearly deleted the module's most interesting features. fasta-cluster-pssm drops a cluster whose consensus is a substring of another's, which is exactly what Rep68 is relative to Rep78 and what VP3 is relative to VP1. Applied as a global rule it emptied REP68. The rule is now scoped per module with the nested sets declared, and all four Rep forms and all three VP forms survive.

The three Rep forms that come from a single annotation string each are a reminder that a feature's evidence base and its importance are unrelated. Rep52 has 93 deposits under one spelling and recalls 47 of 47; Rep78 has 487 deposits under thirty spellings and recalls 312 of 318.
