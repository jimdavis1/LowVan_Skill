Does the grapevine leafroll-associated virus 7 module actually work?

Velarivirus is the smallest of the three closterovirid modules — grapevine leafroll-associated virus 7, cordyline virus 1–4, Actinidia virus 1 and a handful more. It is also the only one carrying a thaumatin-like protein, which no other closterovirid genus has.

Self-recall is a floor, not a validation: a profile that cannot recover its own training data will certainly not recover anything new, but recovering it proves very little. The held-out numbers are in §05 and §06.

02How it was built

Binned from the BV-BRC protein dump, with the accessory names treated as untrustworthy.

The accessories are named by mass, and a mass is not a homology group. Where two masses turned out to be one protein they were merged; where one mass covered two non-homologous groups they were numbered apart.

03Where the cutoffs sit

Conventional for the feature class.

400 for the replicase polyprotein, 300 for the RdRp, 150 for the HSP70 and HSP90 homologs, 80 for the coat proteins, 25–60 for the accessories by length.

35 of the 46 flags are “too many HSPs”, 18 are a missing essential feature. “Genome has too many HSPs” is the dominant flag, and it is a different thing from a duplicate call: it means one feature's profile matched at several places in a genome, not that two features collided. The duplicate-call audit is clean for this module.

07What it cannot do

An earlier report in this session called this module defective. It was wrong. A duplicate-call check without a containment floor reported RDRP as 79% nested inside POLY and the thaumatin-like protein as 66% inside HSP90. Adjacent genes in a compact viral genome overlap routinely; with an 80% containment floor applied, Velarivirus has no exact duplicates and no nested calls at all.

No mature peptides. The replicase is a polyprotein whose cleavage sites are not documented for this genus in a form that can be located from the sequence.

The evaluation is a sample. Measured on a random sample of up to 150 genomes at ≥6 kb (seed 29). Annotation costs about 86 seconds per genome, and 150 is ample for the decision it feeds: the test for copy_num is whether a feature is called on at least 85% of routed genomes, estimated to within about ±6 points at that sample size.

08What the run found

The copy_num correction is the finding. It was invisible until the module was run against genomes, which is the whole argument for measuring essentiality rather than assigning it: a feature believed to be genus-partial turned out to be present on nine genomes in ten.

Seven of seven features have profiles and call at 95.8–100% of their own collections; five are essential on measurement, and the sixth was corrected here.
