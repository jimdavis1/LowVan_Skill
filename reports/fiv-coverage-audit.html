02Where it does not call, and why

orfA at 64.1% is a host-lineage split, not a truncation. Domestic-cat genomes call it 21 of 21; bobcat 0 of 10 and puma 3 of 6. Those same genomes call gag, pol, vif and env 39 of 39, so the absence is profile divergence on a small accessory gene rather than incomplete sequence. Widening orfA to reach the wild felids is a build decision, not a defect.

04Vocabulary saturation

How fast each vocabulary stops growing as genomes are added.

The deposited vocabulary keeps growing; the module's does not. At the depth both reach, 32.9 distinct source strings correspond to 5.0 controlled annotations, a 6.6-fold collapse. The module reaches 95% of its final vocabulary after 14 proteins against the source's 174.

05Method

Coverage is measured by annotating de-replicated exemplars through the real GTO pipeline — annotate_by_viral_pssm-GTO.pl, then the transcript-edit and splice passes, then viral_genome_quality.pl — not by a reimplementation. Spliced features are included, which the flat feature-table path cannot represent. Rates are per exemplar, and exemplars are clustered at 95% nucleotide identity, so one row is one distinct sequence rather than one deposit.
