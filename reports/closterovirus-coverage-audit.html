Does the beet yellows virus module actually work?

Closterovirus is the type genus of the Closteroviridae: beet yellows virus, citrus tristeza virus, carrot yellow leaf virus. Among the longest RNA genomes of any plant virus at up to 19 kb, with a replicase, an RdRp, the quintuple gene block and a long tail of species-specific accessories named by mass.

Self-recall is a floor, not a validation: a profile that cannot recover its own training data will certainly not recover anything new, but recovering it proves very little. The held-out numbers are in §05 and §06.

02How it was built

Binned from the BV-BRC protein dump, with the accessory names treated as untrustworthy.

The accessories are named by mass, and a mass is not a homology group. Where two masses turned out to be one protein they were merged; where one mass covered two non-homologous groups they were numbered apart.

03Where the cutoffs sit

Conventional for the feature class.

400 for the replicase polyprotein, 300 for the RdRp, 150 for the HSP70 and HSP90 homologs, 80 for the coat proteins, 25–60 for the accessories by length.

“Genome has too many HSPs” is the dominant flag, and it is a different thing from a duplicate call: it means one feature's profile matched at several places in a genome, not that two features collided. The duplicate-call audit is clean for this module.

07What it cannot do

CP and CPM cannot be separated on 44% of genomes. They are called on identical coordinates there, and that is biology rather than a defect: the collections share no sequences, but the closterovirid minor coat protein is a duplicated coat protein gene and the two are 32% identical over 74% of their length. Both genes are real, both copy_num assignments are correct, and no cutoff separates the profiles without losing one.

P20 is declared but not essential, and its call rate should not be read at face value — see §05.

The evaluation is a sample. Measured on a random sample of up to 150 genomes at ≥6 kb (seed 29). Annotation costs about 86 seconds per genome, and 150 is ample for the decision it feeds: the test for copy_num is whether a feature is called on at least 85% of routed genomes, estimated to within about ±6 points at that sample size.

The homology rescue has now run on this module. Completed 1 October 2026, closing the item deferred on 30 September. rescue_unassigned.py takes every protein no annotation-string rule matched, blastp’s it against this module’s own collections and adopts each into the feature it matches at ≥80% identity over ≥60% of the query, refusing everything below that rather than sweeping it into a grab-bag. It had never run on this module because it crashed on the trailing tab BV-BRC writes into uniq.seq. 420 of 640 unassigned sequences were adopted; the training set went 1857 → 2091 sequences (+13%) and the profile set 80 → 97. Both the gains and the losses are the same defect, in opposite directions: genomes carrying a duplicate Replicase polyprotein call went 38 → 22. Thirteen genomes that called POLY three times now call it once; six that called it once now call it twice. The cause is the ORF1a/ORF1ab −1 ribosomal frameshift, whose fusion product is binned into POLY alongside ORF1a and cannot be separated by length. The 14 fewer features called are spurious duplicates removed, not coverage lost. This needs transcript_edit references, not more training data. Measured against a control — the shipped module scored on the same 74 genomes with the current JSON — the rescue moved genuinely-clean from 27.0% to 36.5% (13 genome(s) improved, 6 regressed). The earlier comparison against measurements/quality.tsv was discarded: that table predates the 30 September copy_num and vocabulary edits, which on some modules dominate the rescue entirely.

Four features are mass bins holding more than one homology group, and must be split. P18 (CTV 75 | Thesium 2), P19 (GLRaV-2 51 | wheat and Triticum 2), P20 (CTV 54 | BYV 9) and P23 (CTV 94 | Thesium 2 | Raspberry 2) — no pair of groups shares a blastp HSP at 1e-3. P20 is the clearest: CTV p20 is a silencing suppressor and BYV p20 an Hsp70h-interacting vascular-transport protein, and the literature puts CTV p20's real homolog at BYV p21. A bin must be one homology group; until these are split each calls two or more different proteins under one key. Deferred alongside the rescue, because splitting forces a build_collections re-run that discards a rescue — the two are one job.

08What the run found

Two copy_num corrections in opposite directions, from the first measurement of this module against real genomes. One feature had earned essentiality and was not marked; another appeared to have earned it and had not.

Thirteen of thirteen features have profiles, self-recall 93.8–100%, seven essential on measurement, and the one ambiguous case is documented rather than guessed.
