The insect nodaviruses — Flock House, Black beetle, Boolarra, Nodamura, Pariacoto and Newington virus. Two RNA segments, six declared proteins, and the one feature that separates this module from every other nodavirus module: the capsid precursor cleaves itself. Alpha assembles into provirions and then cuts between Asn and Ala, releasing mature beta and the 44-residue gamma peptide (PMID 1404613, PMID 9765417).

What was missed, and why

Two proteins are deliberately absent. The Pariacoto minor capsid protein is real — a virion protein initiated at the second AUG of the capsid ORF (PMID 13679619) — but it is alpha minus 24 N-terminal residues and therefore 100% identical to alpha across its whole length, so no profile can separate the two. The BBV RNA2 second ORF (“URF”, 72 aa) is a 1984 computational prediction with no product reported since and one deposit family-wide.

Beta and gamma are a coordinate operation on alpha, not collections of their own — nothing in BV-BRC is annotated as mature beta. The cut was placed by chemistry on each genome's own precursor rather than at FHV's residue number, because alpha runs 395–407 aa across the genus. Scanning for Asn followed by Ala or Ser with 38–52 residues remaining finds exactly one site in every species and two in none, and it reproduces FHV's published Asn363‖Ala364 without being given it. Pariacoto came out of the same scan as the documented Asn‖Ser exception at gamma 40.
