01What the module covers

One taxon, no outgroup, and a salmon farming problem.

Piscine orthoreovirus is the agent of heart and skeletal muscle inflammation in farmed Atlantic salmon. Every genome record in this dump is PRV; unlike the other orthoreovirus builds there was no sibling clade to separate out. The genome is ten dsRNA segments encoding eleven proteins:

L1  lambda3  ~1,286 aa   RNA polymerase      <- MRV lambda-3   43.2%
L2  lambda2  ~1,290 aa   capping enzyme      <- MRV lambda-2   26.0%
L3  lambda1  ~1,282 aa   core shell          <- MRV lambda-1   32.7%
M1  mu2        ~760 aa   core / cofactor     <- MRV mu-2       22.2%
M2  mu1        ~687 aa   outer capsid        <- MRV mu-1       26.5%
M3  muNS       ~741 aa   non-structural      <- MRV mu-NS      22.3%
S1  sigma3     ~330 aa   outer capsid        BICISTRONIC
    p13        ~124 aa   non-structural
S2  sigma2     ~420 aa   core
S3  sigmaNS    ~354 aa   non-structural      <- MRV sigma-NS   21.5%
S4  sigma1     ~315 aa   cell attachment

The right-hand column is orthology to the shipped Mammalian Orthoreovirus module, used to fix each name to a function rather than trusting the names. They happen to agree here, but the segment numbering does not follow MRV's: the homology rescue placed a bare S4 label into sigma1, which is where PRV puts its attachment protein. At 22–43% identity the four small proteins fall below blastp detection against MRV entirely, and that distance is itself why PRV is its own module.

S1 is bicistronic. 632 genome records carry exactly two proteins and every one of those pairs is p13 with sigma3, so segment S1 encodes both. Unlike Coltivirus segment 9, this is not a read-through: they are two separate ORFs in different frames, and p13 sits entirely inside sigma3's span — 112–486 within 33–1025 on one panel isolate.

02Three sequences put sigma1 on sigma3's gene

The cross-feature audit saw nothing. The panel saw it on 19 of 20 genomes.

On 19 of 20 panel isolates, sigma3 and sigma1 were called at identical coordinates — 1–993 on the same contig, scoring 691.1 and 689.5 bits. A 1.5-bit margin: no cutoff could have separated them.

Pooling both collections and clustering found the cause. Three sequences deposited as sigma1 are really sigma3 — the best cross-label hit is 99.7% identical over 330 aa, and real sigma1 runs 303–315 residues against sigma3's flat 330:

n=170   167 "sigma3" + 3 "sigma1"   all exactly 330 aa   ->  sigma3
n=36     36 "sigma1"               303-315 aa           ->  sigma1

Those three were enough to form a cluster of their own, SIGMA1.2.pssm, built entirely from sigma3 sequences — which then fired on sigma3's locus on every genome that had one. Reassigning them by homology and rebuilding took co-located calls from 19 to 0.

Worth recording how this was caught, because the obvious check missed it. audit_contamination reported no cross-feature contamination before or after: it reassigns a sequence only when it beats its own feature's canonical by 50 bits, and three contaminants forming their own cluster never trigger that, because true sigma3 sequences score about equally against both canonicals. On Avian Orthoreovirus the same audit caught 215 flags, because there the swap ran in both directions. The signature here was visible only in the panel: two features called at identical coordinates on one contig.

04Does it route, and does it call?

The most homogeneous taxon in this family, and the cleanest panel in it.

14 references route 4,582 of 4,582 segment records — 100.0%. Nothing is left uncovered, which has not happened in any other module in this family: Coltivirus needed 23 references for the same result on a far smaller population, and Avian Orthoreovirus leaves 191 records unrouted at 25.
All 20 panel isolates receive all eleven features, with no quality flag of any kind and no feature called twice anywhere. Self-recall against each feature's own in-range collection is 100% on all eleven, and cross-feature contamination is nothing above 50 bits across 629 sequences. copy_num is set on all eleven.

The Met-start gate passes on all 17 alignments, but it took a repair to get there: 227 collection members did not begin with Met. The pattern was a dropped initiator, not a ragged alignment — sigma2 arrives as 19 copies of ARAIFSGISAFF against 18 of MARAIFSGISAF, and lambda2 as 40 of TLYGLRITRLKN against 39 of MATLYGLRITRL. Those were dropped.

The standard N-terminal filter had to be overruled here. Its EXTENDED test wanted to drop 93.5% of sigmaNS and 85% of mu1, because in this taxon a minority carries a genuine upstream Met — 15 copies of MTMGNYQ... extending 50 of MGNYQ..., and for sigmaNS a single longer variant flags 29 of 31. The Met gate never complained about either feature. Only the non-Met members were dropped, which is unambiguous: a collection member that does not start with Met cannot be a CDS start under any reading.

05What it cannot do

Three limits.

sigma1 rests on 36 sequences, the thinnest collection in the module, and it is the feature that was contaminated. It is the cell-attachment protein and therefore the most variable, so it is the one most likely to miss a divergent isolate.

The panel is reassembled, not deposited. No complete PRV genome exists as a single record; each isolate is rebuilt from its own ten segment records, grouped by deposited strain name. Panel contigs are named for their source record rather than a guessed segment, because the three lambdas differ by 8 residues in length and three of the sigmas by under 40.

Only 215 source proteins back the vocabulary comparison. PRV is deposited segment by segment and most records carry one protein, so the rarefaction below is measured on a smaller pool than the collapse figure.

06What the vocabulary bought

Eleven proteins, 91 ways of saying so.

91 distinct annotation strings across 5,069 occurrences collapse onto 11 controlled annotations, an 8.3× reduction — between Coltivirus's 2.5× and Avian Orthoreovirus's 22.2×. Rarefied over proteins at the depth both curves reach, the source holds 41 distinct strings against the module's 11, a 3.7× collapse; the source needs 194 proteins to reach 95% of its vocabulary and the module needs 31.

Two strings had to be left deliberately unbound rather than sent to the not-modelled pile. non-structural protein is deposited in this dump at 124 and 752 residues — p13 and muNS — so it identifies neither by name. Marking it not-modelled would have put 189 real proteins beyond the homology rescue's reach; left unassigned, the rescue placed them correctly, 14 into p13 and 5 into muNS.
