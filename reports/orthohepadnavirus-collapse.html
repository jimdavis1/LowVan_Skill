Orthohepadnavirus

What the annotation cleanup actually collapsed

BV-BRC carries one annotation string per submitter. Seven of this module’s twelve features are derived from those strings, and 363 distinct products collapse to 7 controlled annotations across 195,048 occurrences. The other five features — the spliced protein, the two whole-length isoforms and the two precore peptides — have no deposited vocabulary at all: no submitter annotates them.
