The avian astroviruses — turkey, duck, chicken and wild-bird. Same three-reading-frame layout as the mammalian genus and the same −1 ribosomal frameshift into ORF1b, but nsp1a is about 30% longer: the full-length mode is 1,050–1,099 residues against 800–849 in Mamastrovirus, which is why the two cannot share a length window.

What was missed, and why

ORF1A — Called on 85% of genuine panel genomes against 100% in Mamastrovirus. The misses are divergent lineages: one of them matches the collection at only 27% identity, which is below what 10 profiles built at 80% clustering identity can reach. This is a profile gap, not a threshold, and it is why ORF1A does not carry copy_num.

ORF1AB — 141 references against a correction gate that demands 95% identity. Corrected on 33% of genuine panel genomes.
