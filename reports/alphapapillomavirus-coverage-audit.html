01What the module covers

One circular genome of about 8 kb, read in overlapping frames.

Alphapapillomavirus is the mucosal and cutaneous human genus, and the one that carries the oncogenic types — HPV16, HPV18 and their relatives — alongside the low-risk wart types. The genome is a single circular double-stranded DNA molecule of roughly 8 kb. The module is built on genomes from humans, with a tail of macaque and other primate types.

It declares 14 features: E1, E1E4, E2, E5_ALPHA, E5_BETA, E5_DELTA, E5_GAMMA, E6, E6S, E7, E8E2, L1, L2, L2_MAT. This is the only module of the ten with a split E5. The genus carries four non-homologous E5 proteins — E5α, E5β, E5γ and E5δ — and the deposited annotation string for all of them is the same word.

Excluded from that count: E1E4, E6S, E8E2 — called by an external program and absent from this table, or a protein part of the genus does not encode.

1 of the 7 core features are called on every routed exemplar. The lowest non-spliced rate is E5_DELTA at 0.9%.

The spliced features are called by a separate program on an exact donor/acceptor match, so their rate is a measurement of the junction and not of the profile: E1^E4 on 19.5%, E6* on 1.9%, E8^E2 on 19.4%.

Both spliced rates roughly double — to 45.0% and 44.1% — on the genomes that carry no ambiguous bases. An N anywhere in a donor, an acceptor or the product removes the call, so the raw rate is a property of the deposits.

07What it cannot do

The limits, each measured rather than supposed.

The genus is sequenced clinically, and the deposits show it.

Every mature L2 call in this module begins immediately after a furin site in its own L2 precursor, which is the chemical invariant rather than a fixed offset: furin cleaves after R-X-[KR]-R. Across the family 648 of 681 calls on ambiguity-clean genomes cut after a full site (95.2%).

Use this module on complete Alphapapillomavirus genomes. It calls 7 core features on essentially every genome of the genus; the spliced features are called when the junction is present and readable, and their rate is stated above rather than tuned.

08What the run found

What the build turned up that was not expected.

This is the only module of the ten with a split E5. The genus carries four non-homologous E5 proteins — E5α, E5β, E5γ and E5δ — and the deposited annotation string for all of them is the same word.

The four E5 paralogues cannot be separated by annotation string: the deposits call all of them E5, and that bin holds 9,142 occurrences over 34 strings with no feature behind it. They are separated by position and called by sequence, one profile per orthogroup.
