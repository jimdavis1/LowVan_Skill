What was missed, and why

Three lineages have no L reference and so get no polymerase call: a 6,115 nt Parrot bornavirus 3 contig that cannot hold the gene, Canary bornavirus 1 (its only L deposit does not match its own contig cleanly), and Carlia munda bornavirus, whose L begins at a near-cognate TTG codon.

N' rests on a 1.16× cutoff margin, the narrowest in this module, because it is an exact C-terminal substring of N and no other signal separates them.

Vp1 and Vp2 rest on one genome each, which is every Cartilovirus genome that exists in BV-BRC.

The signal peptide is a prediction and is carried only where it is tight. SignalP 6.0 is installed here and now works -- its model weights had never been converted in and were restored from the licensed tarball already on disk -- but a verified cut is not automatically a good feature. Two clusters were withheld for disagreeing with GP_N about the mature start, and the bit cutoff keeps only calls that tile the precursor exactly. The feature therefore calls on 11 of 36 exemplars rather than all of them.

G_N and G_C have no collection of their own. Nothing in BV-BRC is annotated as a mature bornavirus glycoprotein, so both products were cut out of the G collection at the furin site rather than collected. Richt 1998 (PMID 9557754) places that site after the arginine at position 249 of the 503 aa BoDV-1 precursor; the derivation here finds the single R-X-[K/R]-R match in the 40-62% window and extends it through the basic run, which reproduces residue 249 exactly and generalises to every genus.

L's references are literature-anchored, not deposit-derived. Walker 2000 (PMID 10756058) places intron II at nt 2410-3703 of strain V with the initiator AUG at nt 2393, a 17 nt first exon. Every junction built here was required to round-trip -- cut the reference at its own declared coordinates, translate, and reproduce a clean Met-initiated ORF -- and the measured intron, 1,282-1,294 nt across 107 genomes, reproduces the published 1,294.
