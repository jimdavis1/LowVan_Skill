01What the module covers

Six proteins, four genera, two gene orders — and one glycoprotein that furin cuts in half.

The module covers the whole family Bornaviridae — non-segmented negative-strand RNA viruses of about 8.9 kb that are unique among the Mononegavirales in replicating and transcribing in the nucleus, and in using the host splicing machinery to do it (PMID 8035500, PMID 7831966). Hosts run from horses and humans through parrots and snakes to bony and cartilaginous fish.

Six proteins, one module, two gene orders. Every genus encodes the same six proteins — nucleocapsid (N), X, phosphoprotein (P), matrix (M), glycoprotein (G) and polymerase (L) — but M and G are transposed outside Orthobornavirus: 3'-N-X/P-M-G-L-5' in Orthobornavirus against 3'-N-X/P-G-M-L-5' in Carbovirus and Cultervirus (PMID 42358456, PMID 29462190). Gene order does not enter a profile, so the partitioning question was settled by measurement rather than by taxonomy: 16 reference contigs route 100% of the 218 near-complete genomes of all four genera, so there is no routing gap a split could close, and a Cartilovirus module would hold exactly one genome.

The other five features are products and isoforms rather than genes. N' is the p38 isoform of the nucleocapsid, translated from the second initiation codon (PMID 30541858). G_N and G_C are the two halves of the glycoprotein, cut by furin (PMID 9557754) and known in the literature as GP-N/GP1 and GP-C/GP2. Vp1 and Vp2 are two extra open reading frames that only Cartilovirus carries — ancient duplications of the glycoprotein gene (PMID 38028148).

97.7% of the dump's protein features were bound by rule (3,055 of 3,128), and the homology rescue adopted 27 of the 35 unique sequences left over. The family names its proteins by mass — p40 = N, p10 = X, p24/p23 = P, p16/gp18 = M, p57/gp94 = G — so those strings carry real information and are bound, but only inside an explicit length window. That is what makes it safe: a deposit labelled p24 ORF at 147 aa is not the 201 aa phosphoprotein and never reaches the collection.

Eight of the ten features were built at the documented defaults, -m 5 -mi 0.8 -mc 0.8, with no departure. That was measured rather than assumed: cluster coverage at five or more members runs 79–89% at -mi 0.8 for all six genes, so lowering the identity floor was never necessary and the leftover pass picked up the divergent members at the same floor instead. Twenty-nine of the 71 profiles come from that second pass.

The glycoprotein's two products have no collection of their own. Nothing in BV-BRC is annotated as a mature bornavirus glycoprotein, so both were cut out of the G collection at the furin site rather than collected. Richt 1998 (PMID 9557754) places that site after the arginine at position 249 of the 503 aa BoDV-1 precursor. The derivation finds the single R-X-[K/R]-R match in the 40–62% window and extends it through the basic run, which reproduces residue 249 exactly and generalises across genera: of 154 collection members, 150 carry exactly one such site, with P1 = Arg in 150/150, P4 = Arg in 150/150 and P1′ = Asp in 149/150. The site is enzyme-determined, so it re-locates itself instead of being a column number.

Four truncated phosphoprotein deposits were dropped before any profile existed. check_met_start.py flagged a P cluster whose members begin with Gly; four deposits of 160–182 aa turned out to start 19–40 residues inside the protein, all from one BoDV-1 strain series whose other deposits sit in the unassigned pool as p24 ORF fragments. They passed the length window because they are long enough to. P was rebuilt from 124 members and the check is now clean.

L has no profiles at all. Walker 2000 (PMID 10756058) showed the polymerase depends on splicing, and states the junction for strain V: intron II at nt 2410–3703, initiator AUG at nt 2393, a 17 nt first exon. Measured independently here by requiring every deposited L to match its own genome exactly, 107 orthobornavirus junctions round-trip with a canonical GT..AG, a 17–18 nt first exon and an intron of 1,282–1,294 nt against the published 1,294. The donor and acceptor contexts are strongly conserved — TCATGCGAG|GT in 77 of 107 and TTTCCTTACCAG|CC in 73 — which is what carries the junction to genomes with no deposit. L is collinear in Carbovirus, Cultervirus and Cartilovirus (11 of 11 deposits), so those genera get references with a zero-length intron, which makes the spliced caller return the collinear ORF and keeps the family on one feature. Every reference was round-tripped before shipping; four failed and were dropped.

N' is derived by homology, not by a rule about Met positions. The first attempt cut each N at its own second in-frame Met and disagreed with the profiles on six genomes whose N carries an extra Met at residue 8 — check_met_start.py caught it as two alignments trimmed six residues into their members. The collection is now built by aligning all 129 N sequences, locating the column of BoDV's measured Met14 and cutting at the Met in that column: 112 of 129 qualify, at residue 14 in 81, 16 in 23, 18 in 4, 17 in 2, 15 in 1 and 13 in 1.

Three cutoffs are measurements, not defaults, and each one was set because a false call appeared. At the generated floors of 60 and 30, the single-sequence Vp1 profile fired on the glycoprotein locus of 14 panel genomes — the predictable failure for a profile built from a G paralogue. Measured: Vp1 scores 887 bits on its own Cartilovirus genome and 41–109 on everything else; Vp2 scores 448 against 17–35. Both were set to 200, which is 1.8× above the worst foreign score and 2.2× below the true one, and the false calls went to zero.

G_N needed the same treatment for the same reason. On the one Cartilovirus genome its profiles also fire inside Vp1, which carries its own furin site, giving two G_N calls on one genome. True G_N calls score 103–527 bits across all four genera and that false call peaks at 69, so the floor was set to 85, in the gap. The non_pssm_partner construction was tried first and is worse: call_non_pssm_features pairs every G and G_C HSP, which put 6–16 spurious G_N calls on every exemplar. Tying a product to its precursor is the elegant fix and it is not the one the measurement supports here.

N' needed the most careful cutoff in the module, and it is the narrowest margin here. N' is an exact C-terminal substring of N, so its profiles also match the body of an N that has no second AUG; at the generated cutoff of 60 that produced three calls which did not begin with Met — two carboviruses and Carlia munda bornavirus, none of which encode the isoform. Measured best N' score per exemplar: 25 genuine Met-initiated calls score 510–727 bits, the three false ones 380, 117 and 108, and the genera that lack the isoform entirely score 65–79. bit_cutoff: 440 sits in that gap, 1.16× above the worst false call and 1.16× below the weakest true one. After it: 25 calls, every one Met-initiated and an exact C-terminal substring of its own N, and zero false calls.

L carries no bit cutoff because it carries no profile. It is matched by BLASTn against its 120 references at splice_id / splice_cov of 90/90, also measured: at the shipped defaults of 95/95 the references reach 94.1% of orthobornavirus genomes, at 90/90 they reach 95.1%, and the splice donor and acceptor windows must still match a reference's exactly.

copy_num was set after the module had been run, never before, and on near-complete genomes rather than on the mixed panel — a panel dominated by single-gene amplicons would have marked only M essential, which is an artifact of which partial contigs happen to carry which gene. The six genes clear the 85% bar at 100% (L at 97.2%). The two mature peptides and the two Cartilovirus ORFs deliberately carry no copy_num: mature peptides are not used for genome quality anywhere in LowVan, and a genus-specific accessory marked essential would flag every genome of the other three genera.

Self-recall against each feature's own in-range collection: 1,064 of 1,064 (100%) for the profile features. L is excluded: it has no profiles, and its equivalent check is the round trip every one of its 120 references passed. This is a floor, not a validation; the genome-level figures are in §05 and §06.

Routing is not the constraint in this family, and the reference budget is barely touched. Greedy selection at the annotator's own BLASTn settings reaches every one of the 218 near-complete genomes with four references. Sixteen were shipped rather than four, against a budget of 25: four reach every genome but leave the divergent genera hanging on one marginal hit each, and spreading the selection one reference per species group costs nothing.

The one unrouted genome should stay unrouted. Bactrocera dorsalis borna-like virus is an insect virus of 12,450 nt filed under this family by BV-BRC with no ICTV genus. Scored against the module's profiles directly, only L clears a cutoff (161 bits); N, X, P, M and G all land at 19–24 bits, far below every floor. Adding a seventeenth reference for it would route a genome the module cannot annotate, which converts a visible gap into a silent one.

Every one of the six proteins and both glycoprotein products is called on every routed near-complete genome, in all four genera. L is called on 33 of 36: the three misses are a 6,115 nt contig that cannot physically hold a 5.1 kb gene, Canary bornavirus 1 (whose only L deposit does not match its own contig cleanly, so no junction round-trips) and Carlia munda bornavirus, whose L begins at a near-cognate TTG codon with residues 2–1730 collinear — a non-AUG start this module does not model. N' is called on 25: the 11 genomes without it are the Cultervirus, Carbovirus, Cartilovirus and Carlia munda genomes that genuinely lack a second AUG in the right place.

That did not come for free, and the gap analysis is the reason it is 100% rather than 69%. The first coverage run put X at 25 of 36 and P at 31 — and the misses were not scattered, they were every Cultervirus, Carbovirus and Cartilovirus genome. Scoring each collection member against the installed profiles settled which of the two possible causes it was: the best profile scored 0–26 bits on those deposits against a floor of 30, so it was a coverage hole and not a threshold, and lowering the floor would have bought noise rather than calls. Nine X members and two P members were clustered among themselves at the -mi floor of 0.6 and given their own profiles; three more divergent P deposits followed. X went 69% → 100%, P 86% → 100%.

One protein in this module was recovered by ORF-calling rather than deposited. Carlia munda bornavirus annotates N, P, M, G and L but no X at all. ORF-calling its N→P interval in six frames returned one 78 aa Met-initiated ORF that blasts to the X collection at e = 1.2e-4 and carries the family's VEL[IA]RRL motif. It is kept in Extracted/ so it survives a rebuild.

The cleaved ends are crisp. Checked on all 36 exemplars, G_C starts exactly one base after G_N ends and G_C ends exactly where G ends in 35 of 36; the single exception is one canary bornavirus where G's own start is over-extended by 87 nt and the two products remain internally consistent with each other. qc_duplicate_calls.py reports no exact and no unexpected nested calls; X inside P is the virus's own architecture, since the X open reading frame overlaps the 5′ end of P in a different frame.

97.2% of near-complete genomes come back clean — 35 of 36, with no genome or contig flags at all. The one flagged genome is the truncated 6.1 kb contig, flagged for missing an essential feature, which is the right answer for a genome that is missing it.

Every N′ call was checked against the biological definition, not just against a length: is the called protein Met-initiated, an exact C-terminal substring of the N called on the same genome, and cut 12–17 residues in? 25 of 25 pass, and none fail. The 11 genomes with no N′ call are the Cultervirus, Carbovirus, Cartilovirus and Carlia munda genomes that genuinely lack a second AUG there.

07What it cannot do

One protein's N-terminus cannot be reproduced by a profile, and five described products are deliberately absent.

Three lineages get no polymerase call, and the reason is different in each. Parrot bornavirus 3 1367 is a 6,115 nt contig that cannot physically hold a 5.1 kb gene. Canary bornavirus 1's only L deposit does not match its own contig cleanly, so no junction round-trips and it has no reference. Carlia munda bornavirus begins its L at a near-cognate TTG codon with residues 2–1730 collinear — a non-AUG start this module does not model. That is the price of making L a spliced feature: the call rate falls from 97.2% to 91.7% and the accuracy stops being approximate.

A trap worth recording, because it produced a confident wrong answer. A tblastn collinearity screen of all 745 collection members against their own genomes reported every one as a single collinear HSP, L included, and the first conclusion drawn from it was that nothing in this family is spliced. It is wrong because the genomic frame upstream of the splice acceptor happens to read LCFLTS where the protein reads MSFHAS: five residues out of 1,711, invisible at a 95% identity threshold. The junction was only found by requiring an exact match — and it then reproduced Walker 2000's published coordinates independently.

The glycoprotein signal peptide is not modelled, so G_N is not the fully matured ectodomain. SignalP 6.0 is installed on this machine and answers --help, but its model_weights/ directory contains nothing but a README, so every prediction dies with FileNotFoundError: Fast mode requires model to be installed at .../distilled_model_signalp6.pt. The weights ship inside the licensed tarball and are installed separately by signalp6_convert_models. Until that is done G_N runs from the precursor's own initiator Met and retains the ~20-residue signal peptide. The queries are already staged, so this is one command from being finished.

Five described products are deliberately not features, each for a stated reason. The G/L fusion p165 is real as an mRNA — intron III shares intron II's donor and uses an acceptor at nt 4559, fusing G's first 58 residues to the L frame (PMID 11070091, PMID 11172106) — but it was never detected as a protein, it splices at about 10%, and PMID 11602780 reports that this minor acceptor is not conserved across BoDV isolates. The spliced N isoforms N2/N2′/N3/N3′/N3C of PMID 30541858 were not detectable in infected cells by their own authors, except N3/N3C in a fractionated ER preparation of a recombinant virus. The 1,608 aa and 1,558 aa internal-start forms of L are not modelled: Walker 2000 looked for the 170 kDa one in infected cells and did not find it. Cartilovirus Vp3 and the reptilian cultervirus P–G hybrids are explicitly predictions in their own papers. There is no ‘G′’: the only G-derived proteins in the literature are the precursor and its two furin subunits, both of which are shipped here.

N′ rests on the narrowest cutoff margin in this module. It is an exact C-terminal substring of N, so nothing but a bit score separates them: true calls score 510–727 and the worst false one 380, and the cutoff sits at 440 — 1.16× either way. A divergent orthobornavirus that encodes the isoform but scores below 440 would be missed, and one that lacks it but scores above would be called. That is a real exposure and it is why the figure is reported here rather than buried.

Vp1 and Vp2 rest on one genome each, which is every Cartilovirus genome BV-BRC holds. Their profiles are single-sequence, their cutoffs were set by measuring the one true score against the foreign ones, and nothing else exists to test them on. The avian 'delgebornaviruses' that lack G, X and/or M (PMID 42358456) are not in this dump at all, so the module declares no feature it cannot see.

Use this module on near-complete bornavirus genomes of any genus: routing is 97%, all six proteins, both glycoprotein products and the p38 isoform are called wherever they exist, and every genome comes back clean. Read G_N as the N-terminal furin fragment including its signal peptide rather than as a mature ectodomain, and treat an N′ call on a divergent lineage as resting on a 1.16× margin.

08What the run found

Two defects that only showed up when the module was run on real genomes.

The step-11 pass annotated 37 exemplars, drawn by clustering the 218 near-complete genomes at 95% nucleotide identity, against a routing repository holding only this family and its Mononegavirales neighbours — 67 reference files rather than the repository's full 1,368, which is what keeps a run of this kind inside the voucher ceiling.

Three defects were found by running the module rather than by reading it, and all three were invisible to every collection-level check. The X and P coverage hole in the non-orthobornavirus genera: self-recall was 100%, the length and bin audits were clean, and only annotating real genomes showed that eleven of 36 had no X. The Vp1 false calls on the glycoprotein locus, which no collection check can see because the collection is correct — it is the profile that is promiscuous. And the three Met-less N′ calls, which only appeared when every call was tested against the definition of the isoform rather than against a length window.

The unassigned pool was checked rather than waved through. Two strings reach the member floor, unnamed protein product; p24 ORF and p10 ORF, and neither is a protein this module fails to call: the 147 aa p24 ORF entries begin LPKDLRKNPSMISDPDQRTGREQLSNDELIKKLVTELAEN and the 26 aa p10 ORF entries begin LPKDLRKNPSMISDPDQRTGREQLSN — the same sequence, with no initiator Met, deposited twice under two names. They are fragments of P, and the full-length P of those same strains is already in the collection.

A fourth was found by a tool and not by a person. check_met_start.py reported two N′ alignments that began six residues inside their members; chasing it showed the first N′ derivation — cut each N at its own second in-frame Met — disagreed with the profiles on six genomes whose N carries an extra Met at residue 8. The collection was rebuilt by homology to BoDV's measured Met14 instead, and the check is now clean.

Across the whole dump, 77 distinct BV-BRC annotation strings collapse to the six controlled strings the module emits for the six genes, a 12.8× collapse. The spread is what a mass-naming tradition produces: the nucleocapsid alone arrives as nucleoprotein, N protein, N, p40, P40, p40 protein, p40 nucleoprotein, nucleocapsid protein, helical nucleoside protein and phosphoprotein P40 — the last of which names the wrong protein entirely.

Five of the ten strings this module emits were already in the controlled vocabulary and were reused rather than reinvented: Nucleocapsid protein, Phosphoprotein, Matrix protein, RNA-dependent RNA polymerase and Uncharacterized lineage-specific protein. X protein follows the existing C protein / V protein / W protein convention. The two mature-peptide strings follow the influenza hemagglutinin pattern for a furin-cleaved envelope precursor, and Nucleocapsid protein isoform p38 is new because the isoform is new to this module.

The curve is the more convincing form of the claim, and it is rarefied over proteins rather than genomes, so both curves start at the origin. The module's vocabulary saturates at nine strings after about 92 proteins; the source vocabulary is still climbing at 211 and has reached 29.
